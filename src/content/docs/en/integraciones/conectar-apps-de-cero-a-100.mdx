## In one sentence

**Connecting apps** = making what happens in Brote **show up on its own** in your other tools (your Google spreadsheet, your CRM, your email, your Slack) — and making what happens outside **flow into** Brote on its own. No copy-pasting. No coding.

You already use Brote to chat and sell over WhatsApp. But you're still **hand-copying** every new customer into a spreadsheet, or every payment into your accounting. That's lost time and errors. Connecting apps makes that work **happen by itself**: new customer in Brote → new row in your spreadsheet + welcome email, all automatic.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"Every time something happens in Brote (a new customer, a payment, a hot lead), how do I get it **automatically** recorded in my other apps without touching anything?"
</Aside>

**Plainly put:** it's for the business that already has **more than one tool** and is tired of copy-pasting between them.

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **Your Brote account up and running** with at least one channel connected (WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.) — meaning conversations are already coming in.

2. **An account in the destination tool** (Google Sheets, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Notion, Slack… whatever you use).

3. **The right plan** for your path:
   - Easy path → a **Zapier** account (they have a free plan to start) + your Brote plan on **Pro** or **Business**.
   - Advanced path → **Make**, **n8n**, or your own server + Brote **Business** plan.

4. **A Brote API key**, generated in one click from `Settings → Integrations`. It's the "key" that connects Brote to the other tool.

5. Being clear on **what you want to automate** (e.g., "every new customer → into my spreadsheet"). With that, you can build your first flow.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
You don't need to know how to code. With Zapier everything is point-and-click. The API key is generated with a button — no need to understand what it is under the hood.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Rodrigo 🌱

> **Rodrigo** runs **"Fitness Andes"**, a personal training studio in Santiago. He acquires clients over WhatsApp with his Brote AI agent. The problem: he was logging every new client **by hand** in a Google spreadsheet and sending the welcome email **one by one**. That cost him around 10 minutes per client, and sometimes he forgot.

<Steps>

1. **Rodrigo opens Zapier** (with his free account) and creates a new "Zap". A Zap is a rule like "when THIS happens, do THAT".

2. **He picks the trigger:** he searches for "Brote" in Zapier, connects his account by pasting the **API key**, and picks the **`create_contact`** event — i.e., "when a new contact is created in Brote".

3. **He picks the first action:** "Google Sheets → add row". He connects his "Clientes Fitness Andes" spreadsheet and maps the fields: name, phone, date. **Without writing a single line of code** — just picking from a list.

4. **He adds a second action:** "Gmail → send email". He writes the welcome email once ("Welcome to Fitness Andes! Your first class is…") and tells it to use the new contact's name and email.

5. **He tests the Zap:** Zapier sends a test row to the spreadsheet and a test email. It works. Rodrigo turns the Zap on.

6. **Done, it works on its own forever.** From that moment, everyone who signs up over WhatsApp **automatically** appears in the spreadsheet and receives their welcome email — without Rodrigo touching anything.

</Steps>

**Before vs. after (with 40 new clients per month):**

| | Before (by hand) | After (automatic) |
|---|---|---|
| Time per client | ~10 min | 0 min |
| Time per month | ~6.5 hours | 0 hours |
| Clients he forgot | 3-4 per month | 0 |
| Welcome email | Sometimes yes, sometimes no | Always, instantly |

**Result:** Rodrigo gets back **over 6 hours a month** (almost a full workday) and **no client goes unrecorded or unwelcomed**. He spends those 6 hours teaching classes, which is what makes him money.

## How easy is it?

**With Zapier: very easy.** It's point-and-click. There's no code at any step. Building your first Zap takes about 10-15 minutes the first time, and after that it's a matter of minutes. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can build a Zap.

**With Make or n8n: easy-to-intermediate.** Same idea as Zapier (dragging blocks), but built for more sophisticated flows (with conditions, loops, transformations). Ideal if you already use them or want something very custom.

**With webhooks + direct API: this one's for developers.** If you have your own system (an ERP, a custom-built CRM), your technical team connects Brote through our API. It's the most powerful path and also the most technical.

<Aside type="tip">
Just getting started? Go with **Zapier**. It covers 90% of cases without writing anything. The other paths are for when you outgrow it.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know how to code?**
With **Zapier, no.** Everything is picking from lists and clicking. Coding is only needed if you go the webhooks/API route with your own system — and in that case your technical team does it, not you.

**What's the difference between Zapier and webhooks?**
**Zapier** is the no-code way: you connect Brote with 6,000+ well-known apps by pointing-and-clicking, available from the **Pro** plan. **Webhooks + API** are the direct, technical way: Brote sends events to your server and you program what to do, available on the **Business** plan. Zapier for most people; webhooks for custom integrations.

**What about Make or n8n?**
They use the same infrastructure as webhooks (which is why they require **Business**), but give you a visual interface to build advanced flows without writing as much code. Great if you need conditions, loops, or data transformations along the way.

**Is the connection secure?**
Yes. Brote signs every outgoing event with **HMAC-SHA256** (a signature guaranteeing the data really comes from Brote and nobody forged it). And the API key is your private key — it's shown only once, so store it safely.

**Is the Brote app on Zapier public yet?**
It's in **Zapier's public review** and available as a **private app** in the meantime. Ask us for the invite at [hola@brote.ai](mailto:hola@brote.ai) and you can connect it just the same. It's in production (v1.0.2), stable and validated with active customers.

**How long does it take to get running?**
Your first Zapier flow is up and running in about 10-15 minutes. From there it runs on its own, no maintenance.
