## In one sentence

**Google Sheets + Brote** = every new person who messages your agent (with their name, phone, email, interest, channel, and date) **lands on its own in a Google spreadsheet**, ready for reports or for the team that works in Sheets/Excel.

Your AI agent serves customers all day over WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messenger. That flow of contacts and sales stays inside Brote — but **your admin team, your accountant, or your head of sales live in a spreadsheet**. Until now, someone hand-copied each new customer's data into Sheets. With this integration, **Brote writes the row on its own**: new contact → new row, in real time. Zero copy-paste.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"My agent handles conversations and sells, but my team builds reports in a Google spreadsheet. How do I get **every new customer to show up in Sheets on its own**, without copying anything by hand?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| Your team **builds reports in Google Sheets / Excel** | You already work entirely inside Brote and don't need a spreadsheet |
| You want a **shared record** of leads that fills itself | You manage your catalog in Shopify and don't need a sheet |
| You have people (admin, accountant, sales) who **don't log into Brote** | You work alone and don't share data with anyone |
| You want to **report without depending on whoever copies by hand** | You'd rather export a CSV every now and then and be done |

**Plainly put:** it's for the business whose **admin team lives in a spreadsheet** and wants Brote's customers and sales to land there on their own, without keying in data.

<Aside type="note" title="Plan required: Starter (Brote) 🌱">
The Google Sheets integration is available **from the Starter plan**. It's not in the Mini (sampler) plan. With Starter or higher, your agent can read your spreadsheet as a catalog **and** write every new contact into it.
</Aside>

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **A Google account** with access to Google Drive (the one you already use for your spreadsheets).

2. **A Google Sheets spreadsheet** created — it can be empty. You'll define columns like `Nombre`, `Email`, `Teléfono`, `Origen`. If you also want to use the sheet as a catalog, add a second tab (`catalog_tab`) with `Producto`, `Precio`, `Stock`.

3. **A channel connected in Brote** (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger…) — that's where the contacts that will be written into the spreadsheet come from.

4. An active **Starter plan** or higher.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
When you connect, Brote gives you a **service email** (something like `...@brote.iam.gserviceaccount.com`). You share your spreadsheet with that email in **Editor** mode — that way Brote can write rows. Nothing technical needed: it's the same "Share" button you already use in Google Sheets.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Rodrigo 🌱

> **Rodrigo** runs **"Clima Norte"**, an air conditioning installation company in La Serena. His AI agent handles quotes over WhatsApp and Instagram. But his sister **Paula**, who runs the admin side, keeps everything in a Google spreadsheet shared with the accountant and the two field technicians. Every morning, Paula hand-copied the day's new customers from her phone. She lost 40 minutes and sometimes missed one.

<Steps>

1. **Rodrigo connects Google Sheets in Brote** in 3 minutes: he creates a "Leads Clima Norte" spreadsheet with columns `Fecha`, `Nombre`, `Teléfono`, `Interés`, `Canal`, shares it with Brote's service email, and pastes the URL in Settings → Integrations → Google Sheets. He maps which column holds which value. Done.

2. **Camila messages him on WhatsApp** on a Tuesday at 9 PM: *"Hola, quiero cotizar un split de 12.000 BTU para mi living."*

3. **Brote's AI agent handles her**: it asks for her details, gives her a price range, and books a technical visit. Quietly, Brote captures: name "Camila", phone, interest "split 12.000 BTU", channel "WhatsApp".

4. **Automatically, Brote adds a row** to Paula's spreadsheet: `05-07-2026 · Camila · +56 9… · Split 12.000 BTU · WhatsApp`. Without anyone touching anything.

5. **The next morning, Paula opens the spreadsheet** and the 6 leads that came in overnight are already there — with date, channel, and what each one asked for. She copied none of them.

6. **The accountant and the technicians see the same sheet live**: the technicians filter by "visit booked", the accountant sums up the month's closed deals. Everyone works on the same spreadsheet, which fills itself.

</Steps>

**Rodrigo and Paula never hand-copied a contact again.**

## How easy is it?

Easy. The connection takes **about 3 minutes** and only requires sharing your spreadsheet:

<Steps>

1. Create or open your spreadsheet in **Google Drive**.

2. In Brote, go to **Settings → Integrations → Google Sheets** — copy the **service email** it shows you.

3. In your spreadsheet, tap **Share**, paste that email, and give it **Editor** permission.

4. Back in Brote, paste your **spreadsheet's URL** → Brote detects the columns → you **map** which one is `Nombre`, `Email`, `Teléfono`, `Origen`. ✅

5. Test it: add a row by hand, wait a few seconds, and ask the agent to record a test contact.

</Steps>

After that, **you do nothing else**. Every new contact the agent handles lands in the sheet on its own.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Brote need my Google password?**
No. You share the spreadsheet with a **service email** (a technical Brote account) in Editor mode. Brote can only edit the spreadsheets you've shared with it — nothing else in your Drive.

**Can I use the same spreadsheet as a catalog AND for leads?**
Yes. In the MVP we support **one spreadsheet per organization**, but you can set up **two tabs** in the same sheet: a `catalog_tab` (for the agent to read prices and stock) and a `leads_tab` (where it writes new contacts). Brote recognizes them separately.

**How fast does a new contact appear in the sheet?**
A new lead is written the moment it's captured. For catalog **reads**, Brote caches the spreadsheet for 5 minutes (there's a "Refresh now" button if you changed a price and don't want to wait).

**Does it work if my spreadsheet is huge?**
Up to **5,000 rows** it runs smoothly. If your catalog is bigger than that, an e-commerce integration like Shopify is a better fit. For leads/reports, you're unlikely to hit that size.

**What if I'd rather send the data to another tool (my CRM, another spreadsheet, a system)?**
Google Sheets is the direct record. If you want to send the same data to **other systems** (your CRM, email, notifications), you build that with automations like Zapier or Make/n8n — same idea, more destinations.

**Do I need a paid plan?**
Yes, from **Starter**. The Google Sheets integration isn't in the Mini plan.
