## In one sentence

**Syncing Google Calendar** = connecting your Google calendar to Brote so that **the appointments your AI agent books over WhatsApp show up in your calendar on their own**, and so that **the agent never offers a time slot where you already have something**.

You already live in your Google Calendar. With this connection, every appointment the agent books gets written there automatically, and every event you have (a meeting, lunch, a personal block) tells the agent *"don't book here."* **One single calendar, no clashes, no copying appointments by hand.**

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"The agent books appointments for me over WhatsApp… but how do I make sure it **doesn't clash** with the meetings I already have in my Google Calendar, and that I **don't have to copy them** one by one?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You already use **Google Calendar** day to day | You don't use a digital calendar |
| Your AI agent **books appointments over WhatsApp** (you use Brote's scheduling) | You don't take appointments or bookings |
| You have **personal events or meetings** you don't want stepped on | Your Brote calendar and your life never overlap |
| You're a professional or business with a calendar: **physical therapist, dentist, lawyer, stylist, consultant, real estate, hospitality** | You serve walk-ins with no calendar |

**Plainly put:** it's for anyone who **already organizes their life in Google Calendar** and wants WhatsApp appointments to flow into that same calendar, without duplicating work or risking double bookings.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: Starter (Brote) and up">
Google Calendar sync is part of **scheduling**, available **from the Starter plan**. Connecting the calendar itself has no extra cost and isn't billed separately — it's included in your plan. On Mini you'll see scheduling with a plan-required notice; it activates when you upgrade to Starter or higher.
</Aside>

3. **A Google account** with the calendar you use day to day (personal Gmail or your business's Google Workspace).

4. An active **Starter plan** (Brote) or higher.

</Steps>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Dr. Rojas 🦷

> **Dr. Rojas** runs a **dental practice** and already organizes her whole life in Google Calendar: meetings with the lab, lunch, her daughter's classes, a conference on Thursday. Her Brote AI agent was already taking appointments over WhatsApp, but at first it **didn't know about her Google Calendar** — so one Tuesday it booked a cleaning at 11:00… right when she had a meeting with the prosthetics technician. Double booking. Here's how she fixed it by connecting the calendar.

<Steps>

1. **Dr. Rojas goes to Settings → Scheduling** and taps **"Connect Google Calendar"**. She authorizes with her Google account in the window that appears. It takes less than 1 minute.

2. **Brote creates a new calendar called "Brote — Citas"** inside her Google account, separate from her main calendar. That's where the agent's appointments will land, neatly organized and apart from her personal calendar.

3. **Dr. Rojas picks which calendar(s) Brote reads for her availability.** She checks her main calendar → now the agent knows the conference Thursday and the lab meetings are busy.

4. **Camila messages the practice's WhatsApp:** *"Hola, ¿tienen hora para una limpieza esta semana?"*. The agent checks the doctor's real availability (free/busy), **rules out the conference Thursday and the Tuesday 11:00 meeting**, and replies: *"Hi! I have Tuesday 3:30 PM or Wednesday 10:00 AM. Which works for you?"*

5. **Camila replies "Wednesday 10:00".** The agent books it, confirms over WhatsApp, and **writes the appointment into the "Brote — Citas" calendar**. Dr. Rojas touches nothing: she opens her Google and there's Wednesday's 10:00 cleaning, with Camila's name.

6. **That same afternoon Dr. Rojas blocks Friday** in her Google Calendar because she's leaving early. The agent respects it instantly: when another customer asks for a Friday slot, **the agent no longer offers it**. No telling anyone, no editing Brote.

</Steps>

**A month later, Dr. Rojas does the math:**

| | Before (not connected) | With Google Calendar connected |
|---|---|---|
| Appointments clashing with meetings | 2–3 per month | 0 |
| Copying appointments from WhatsApp to Google | One by one, by hand | Automatic |
| Blocking personal time | The agent booked over it anyway | The agent respects it on its own |
| Her daily calendar | In two places (Brote + Google) | In one place (Google) |

**The math that convinced her:** every clashing appointment cost her an awkward reschedule with the patient and ~30 minutes of back-and-forth over WhatsApp. She went from 2–3 clashes a month to **zero**, and stopped hand-copying every appointment that came in through chat. All for authorizing her Google account once.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
One single calendar. WhatsApp appointments **land on their own** in your Google, and everything already in Google **puts the brakes** on the agent. Zero double bookings, zero copy-paste.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. It's **one click**: go to Settings → Scheduling, tap "Connect Google Calendar", authorize with your account, and you're done. Brote creates the "Brote — Citas" calendar on its own, and you choose which calendar to read for your availability. No copying codes, no technical setup.

<Aside type="note" title="How long does it take to get running?">
Less than 2 minutes, and you only do it once. From then on it works on its own: every new appointment goes into your Google, and every event of yours blocks that slot.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it touch or mess up my main calendar?**
No. Brote **doesn't write to your main calendar**. It creates a separate calendar called **"Brote — Citas"** that only holds the appointments the agent books. Your personal calendar stays intact. From your main calendar, Brote only **reads** your availability so it doesn't book over you.

**Does Brote see the contents of my personal events?**
For your availability, Brote uses **free/busy** mode: it cares whether a window is **busy or free**, not the details of what you have. It only writes new events (the appointments) into the "Brote — Citas" calendar.

**Can I read multiple calendars?**
Yes. You can pick several calendars for your availability — for example "Citas Doctora Rojas" and "Visitas Inmuebles Norte" — and the agent respects all of them before offering a slot.

**If I block time in my Google, does the agent stop offering it?**
Yes, instantly. Add an event to a calendar Brote reads and that slot stops appearing among the available times. It's the simplest way to "close off" time from the agent.

**Do I absolutely need Google Calendar to use scheduling?**
No. Brote's scheduling works fine without connecting Google. But connecting it gets you two things: appointments land in your usual calendar, and the agent never books over your existing events.

**What if I use Outlook or Apple Calendar?**
For now, direct sync is with **Google Calendar**. If you use another one, Brote's scheduling keeps working (with its own internal calendar), but automatic sync is Google-only.
