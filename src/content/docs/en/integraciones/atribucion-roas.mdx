## In one sentence

**Attribution** = knowing **which ad generated each sale**, even if the sale closes on WhatsApp days after the click.

You spend money on Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok ads. Those ads bring people who message you, chat with your AI agent, and end up buying. **Without attribution, you don't know which ad worked** — you only see "I spent $200.000 and sold something." With attribution, you know exactly: *"the red dress ad brought me 8 sales of $40.000 each."*

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"I spent $X on ads this month. How much did I sell **thanks to them**, and **which one** performed best?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You invest in **Meta (Instagram/Facebook)** or **TikTok** ads | You don't run paid ads |
| Your ads lead to a **conversation** ("Send message" button / Click-to-WhatsApp) | You only sell in person, without ads |
| You collect payments **online** (Mercado Pago, Flow, Khipu, Stripe…) | You don't collect payments through Brote / don't record payments |

**Plainly put:** it's for the entrepreneur or business that **runs ads and wants to know if that money pays off**.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: Business (Bosque)">
Sales attribution is a **Business plan exclusive**. It's Brote's most advanced feature (it closes the ROAS loop + feeds Meta/TikTok optimization), built for businesses already investing in ads at volume. If you're on Mini/Starter/Pro, you'll see the card with a lock — it activates when you upgrade to Business.
</Aside>

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **A business already running paid ads** on Meta or TikTok (or about to start).

2. **An advertiser account** on the platform:
   - **Meta:** a [Business Manager](https://business.facebook.com/) with a **Pixel** created (in Events Manager).
   - **TikTok:** a [TikTok Ads Manager](https://ads.tiktok.com/) account with a **Pixel** created (in Events Manager).

3. **Ads that lead to a message** — the famous *Click-to-WhatsApp*, *Click-to-Instagram*, or *Click-to-Messenger*. These are ads with a "Send message" button instead of "Buy now".

4. **An official channel connected in Brote** (official WhatsApp Cloud, Instagram, or Messenger) — that's where the customer comes in and Brote captures which ad they came from.

5. **A payment provider connected** (Mercado Pago, Flow, Khipu, Stripe, or PayPal) — it's what fires the conversion event when the customer pays.

6. An active **Business plan**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Don't have a Pixel yet? You can create one for free in 5 minutes in Meta's or TikTok's Events Manager. It's the "sensor" that receives your conversion data.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with María 🌱

> **María** runs **"Ropa Aurora"**, an online clothing store on Instagram. She sells over WhatsApp. This month she decided to invest in ads.

<Steps>

1. **María creates a Click-to-WhatsApp ad** on Instagram: a photo of a red dress, with a "Send message" button. Budget: $150.000 per month.

2. **Ana sees the ad** in her feed, loves the dress, and taps "Send message". WhatsApp opens with "Hola, me interesa el vestido rojo 👗".
   *(Meta attaches an invisible code to that message — the `ctwa_clid` — identifying which ad Ana came from.)*

3. **Brote's AI agent replies** to Ana: it shows her sizes, colors, the price ($40.000), and sends her a **Mercado Pago payment link**.
   *(Quietly, Brote has already saved on Ana's contact: "came from the red dress ad".)*

4. **Ana pays** the $40.000 through the link. Mercado Pago approves the payment.

5. **Automatically**, Brote tells Instagram/Meta: *"the person who came from the red dress ad bought $40.000"*. Without María touching anything.

6. **Meta connects the dots:** that red dress ad generated a $40.000 sale. The same happens with the other 7 people who bought that month.

</Steps>

**At the end of the month, María opens her Events Manager (or Brote) and sees:**

| Ad | Spend | Attributed sales | ROAS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Red dress | $80.000 | 8 sales · $320.000 | **4x** 🎉 |
| 🔵 Blue jeans | $70.000 | 1 sale · $35.000 | 0.5x 😕 |

**María's decision (in 30 seconds):** *"The red dress ad returns 4 times what I invest → I put MORE money into it. The jeans ad loses money → I turn it off."* Without attribution, María would have kept spending on the jeans ad without knowing it wasn't working.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
You stop running ads "blind". Every ad dollar becomes **measurable** → you put money where it performs and cut what doesn't. That is literally **earning more while selling the same**.
</Aside>

## Which metrics will you get and what are they for?

| Metric | What it is | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Attributed sales** | How many sales came from each ad | Knowing which ad works |
| **Attributed value ($)** | How much money each ad generated | Calculating whether the ad pays off |
| **ROAS** (Return On Ad Spend) | How much you earn per dollar invested | The king metric: above 1 = you win, below 1 = you lose |
| **Cost per conversion** | How much each sale cost you in ads | Comparing channels/ads |
| **Events sent** | How many conversions Brote reported to Meta/TikTok | Verifying the integration is working |

**The most powerful part:** these metrics aren't just for *looking at*. Meta and TikTok use them to **optimize your ad spend automatically** — their algorithm learns "this person bought" and goes out to **find more people like them** to show your ad to. **You feed the algorithm better fuel → it brings you better customers → you spend less per sale.**

## How do you connect it? (this easy)

There are **two ways**. The 1-click option is the easiest.

### Option A — 1-click connection (recommended)

<Steps>

1. In Brote: **Settings → Integrations → Attribution**.

2. On the **Meta** (or TikTok) card, tap **"Connect with 1-click"**.

3. A Meta/TikTok window opens → **you authorize your pixel** with your account.

4. Done. Brote discovers your pixel on its own and it's **connected** ✅. No copying codes.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="How long does it take?">
Less than 1 minute. You don't need to know anything technical — just click "yes" in the Meta/TikTok window.
</Aside>

### Option B — Manual connection (if you prefer pasting the values)

<Steps>

1. In Meta/TikTok's **Events Manager**, copy your **Pixel ID** and generate an **Access Token** (Conversions API → Generate token).

2. In Brote: **Settings → Integrations → Attribution** → paste Pixel ID + Access Token → **Connect**.

3. (Optional) Paste a **Test Event Code** to validate without affecting your reports.

</Steps>

## And after connecting? You do nothing else

That's the magic: **you connect once and it works on its own forever.** Every time a customer who came from an ad pays, Brote reports the conversion automatically. There's nothing to configure per ad or per sale.

Also, in the **Inbox**, contacts who came from an ad show a **"🎯 Came from a Meta Ad"** badge — so your team knows that customer cost ad money and prioritizes them.

## Meta vs TikTok — which one do I connect?

Connect **the platform(s) where you run ads**. You can have both at once.

| | Meta (Instagram/Facebook) | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Entry channels | WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Messenger | TikTok ads → message |
| Conversion event | Automatic `Purchase` on payment | Automatic `CompletePayment` on payment |
| Status in Brote | ✅ Available | ✅ Available |

## Frequently asked questions

**Do Meta/TikTok charge me for this?**
No. The Conversions API / Events API is **free**. You only pay for your ads as usual.

**Is it hard?**
No. With 1-click, you authorize your pixel and that's it. The "hard" part (having an ads account + pixel + ads) is something you already have if you run ads.

**Is it useful if I don't sell much yet?**
Attribution shines when **you invest in ads at some volume**. That's why it's a Business plan feature. If you're just starting, focus first on connecting your WhatsApp and building your AI agent.

**What if I don't use Brote's payment providers?**
The conversion event fires when a connected provider (MP/Flow/Khipu/Stripe/PayPal) approves a payment. If you collect payments elsewhere, the sale isn't attributed automatically.
