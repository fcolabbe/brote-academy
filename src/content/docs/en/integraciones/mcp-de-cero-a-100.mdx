## In one sentence

**MCP** = connecting Brote to **Claude or ChatGPT** so you can ask questions about your business **in natural language**, and the AI answers with **your real Brote data**.

Instead of opening Brote, going into the Inbox, filtering, and counting by hand, you write to Claude *"how many sales did I close this week?"* or *"show me the contacts who chatted but didn't buy"* — and it answers right away, reading straight from your Brote. It's like having an assistant who knows all your customers and your numbers.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"I use Claude/ChatGPT every day. Can I ask it about **my business** — my sales, my customers, my conversations — without opening Brote?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You already use **Claude** (from Anthropic) or **ChatGPT** day to day | You don't use AI assistants yet |
| You want to ask the AI about **your business** without opening Brote | You prefer looking at your data inside Brote (dashboard, Inbox) |
| You're comfortable writing to an assistant in natural language | You're not interested in "conversing" with your data |

**Plainly put:** it's for the owner who already lives inside Claude or ChatGPT and wants their assistant to also know their business — their customers, sales, and conversations — so they can ask instead of searching by hand.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: Business (Bosque)">
Connecting Brote to Claude/ChatGPT via MCP is a **Business plan exclusive**. It's an advanced capability (your AI assistant can read your Inbox, contacts, and payments, and even act on them), built for businesses operating at volume. If you're on Mini/Starter/Pro, you'll see the option with a lock — it activates when you upgrade to Business.
</Aside>

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **Claude Desktop or ChatGPT** installed or open — the AI assistant you already use. (Claude Desktop is Anthropic's desktop app; ChatGPT supports custom connectors).

2. **A Brote account with real data** — conversations, contacts, payments. If your Brote is empty, the AI won't have anything to tell you.

3. **A Brote API key** — it's like a "key" that gives your assistant permission to read your Brote. You generate it in one click from **Settings → Integrations** and copy it once.

4. An active **Business plan**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
You don't need to know how to code. You copy a key from Brote and paste it into your assistant's settings — just once. After that, you talk to it in plain language, like any chat.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Daniela 🌱

> **Daniela** runs **"Deco Nube"**, a home decor store that sells over WhatsApp and Instagram. Every day she uses Claude to draft copy and organize her ideas. She'd love to ask it about her business without having to open Brote and look at reports.

<Steps>

1. **Daniela generates her key** in Brote: she goes to **Settings → Integrations**, taps **"Generate API key"**, and copies the token (it appears only once).

2. **She connects it to Claude once.** She pastes the key into Claude Desktop's settings following Brote's step-by-step. Restarts Claude. Done — Claude now "sees" her Brote.

3. **In the morning, instead of opening Brote, she asks Claude:** *"From Brote, who are my top 10 customers this month?"* Claude reads her real Brote and answers with names, amounts, and how much each one bought.

4. **She keeps chatting in natural language:**
   - *"How many sales did I close this week, and for how much?"*
   - *"Show me the contacts who chatted but didn't buy."*
   - *"Which conversations do I have open and unanswered?"*
   - *"How am I doing on messages vs. last week?"*

5. **Claude doesn't just read — it can also act** (if Daniela asks): *"Generate a $35.000 payment link for the wicker lamp"* or *"Mark Pedro González as a customer"*. Brote executes and confirms.

</Steps>

**What changed for Daniela:** before, she'd open Brote, go into the Inbox, filter by date, and count by hand. Now she asks Claude — the assistant she already had open — and gets the answer in seconds, with **real data from her business**, not made up.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
Your favorite AI assistant stops being generic and starts **knowing your business**. You ask it about your customers, sales, and conversations like you'd ask it anything else — and it answers with the truth from your Brote.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

The connection is done **once** and keeps working forever.

<Steps>

1. **Generate your key** in Brote (one click, copy it).

2. **Paste it into your assistant** (Claude Desktop or ChatGPT), following the step-by-step — it's copy and paste, no code.

3. **Restart the assistant** and that's it: ask it whatever you want about your business.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="How long does it take?">
The connection takes a few minutes the first time (copying the key and pasting it). After that, every question gets answered in seconds. If you want the exact technical step-by-step for Claude Desktop, it's in the detailed guide (link below).
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Your key is like a password">
The key you generate gives your assistant permission to **read your Inbox, contacts, and payments, and even send messages or generate charges**. Treat it like a password: don't share it. If it leaks, revoke it in one click from Settings → Integrations and generate a new one.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. You generate a key in Brote and paste it into your assistant. It's copy and paste. After that, you talk to it in plain language.

**Does it work with ChatGPT as well as Claude?**
Yes. It works with **Claude Desktop** (from Anthropic) and with **ChatGPT** via its custom connectors. Also with your own AI agents, if you have one.

**Does the AI make up the data?**
No. The whole point is that the AI reads **straight from your real Brote** — your sales, your contacts, your conversations. It doesn't guess: it queries the source. That's why you connect your key.

**Can the AI do things or only look?**
Both. By default you use it to ask (read). But if you explicitly ask, it can also act: generate a payment link, mark a contact as a customer, send a message. You're in charge.

**Is it useful if my Brote is brand new?**
It shines when you already have activity — conversations, customers, sales. If you're just starting, focus first on connecting your WhatsApp and building your AI agent; this feature stands out when there's data to query.

**Why is it a Business plan feature?**
Because you're giving an external assistant access to your entire operation (Inbox, contacts, payments, logistics). It's an advanced capability built for businesses already operating at volume.
