If your business doesn't have Shopify but you do maintain a Google Sheet with products/prices/stock, Brote can read it live. The agent checks the sheet when a customer asks about price or availability.

## Two usage modes

### Read mode — Sheet as a catalog

The agent reads your sheet to answer catalog questions:

- **Customer asks** "how much is the XL box?"
- **Agent looks up** the row in the sheet → finds price + stock
- **Replies** "The XL box costs $45.000 and we have 8 in stock"

Useful when:
- You don't have Shopify or an e-commerce store
- Your catalog changes 1-2 times a week (doesn't need real e-commerce)
- You're a service business (pricing by the hour, by package) rather than products

### Write mode — Sheet as a lightweight CRM

Brote writes to the sheet when it captures a new lead:

- **A new customer** enters the chat → the agent captures name, email, phone, interest.
- **Brote adds a row** to your sheet with timestamp + channel + tags.
- Useful when your team doesn't use Brote — they can watch leads from the sheet in real time.

## How to connect

1. **Create or open your sheet** in Google Drive.
2. **Share it** with Brote's service email (we give it to you when connecting) with Editor permission.
3. In Brote: **Settings → Integrations → Google Sheets** → paste the sheet's URL.
4. Brote detects the columns → you map which one is "Producto", "Precio", "Stock", "Categoria" for read mode. Map "Nombre", "Email", "Teléfono", "Origen" for write mode.
5. **Test it**: add a row manually, wait 30 seconds, ask the agent.

<Aside type="tip" title="One sheet per org">
In the MVP we support **one sheet per organization**. If you keep catalog + leads in separate sheets, merge them into a single Sheet with two tabs (`catalog_tab` and `leads_tab`) — Brote reads them separately but knows both.
</Aside>

## Performance

- **Cache**: Brote caches reads for 5 minutes. If you update a price, the agent sees it after 5 min at most.
- **Force refresh**: there's a "Refresh now" button in the settings if you want to force a reload.
- **Large sheets**: up to 5,000 rows runs smoothly. If your catalog is bigger, consider Shopify.
