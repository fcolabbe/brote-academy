## Triggers (Brote → Zapier)

When something happens in Brote, Zapier receives it signed with HMAC and fires the action you choose:

- **`new_conversation`** — A new conversation starts
- **`payment_received`** — Payment confirmed via Mercado Pago
- **`ticket_won`** — Ticket closed as won
- **`handoff_to_human`** — The AI agent passes the conversation to a human

## Actions (Zapier → Brote)

Zapier can invoke actions in Brote:

- **`send_message`** — Sends a WhatsApp/Webchat message from an external trigger
- **`create_contact`** — Creates a contact in Brote from a form/CRM
- **`qualify_lead`** — Marks a contact as a hot lead
- **`add_note`** — Adds an internal note to a conversation
- **`move_ticket_stage`** — Moves a ticket to another pipeline stage
- **`tag_conversation`** — Adds a tag to the conversation

## Typical use cases

### Mercado Pago payment → invoice in Holded
When a customer pays via a link generated by the agent, Zapier copies the amount to your accounting system.

### Qualified lead → CRM
The AI agent marks a hot lead → it shows up as a deal in HubSpot/Pipedrive/Salesforce with all the data.

### Handoff → Slack
When the customer asks to talk to a human, a ping lands in #ventas with the full context.

### Spreadsheet → Brote contacts
Import your Google Sheets base into Brote as contacts with tags ready for a campaign.

## Setting up

1. **In Brote**: go to `Settings → Integrations` and generate an **API key**.
2. **In Zapier**: search for "Brote" in the app directory.
3. **Connect your account** by pasting the API key.
4. Create your first Zap using triggers or actions.

[Go to Zapier →](https://zapier.com)
