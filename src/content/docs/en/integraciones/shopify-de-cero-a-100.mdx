## In one sentence

**Shopify + Brote** = your AI agent knows **your real catalog** (products, prices, stock) and builds the **cart link** so the customer buys without leaving the chat.

You already have your store set up on Shopify. The problem is that people message you on WhatsApp asking "do you have this?", "how much is it?", "is there one left for me?" — and someone on your team has to go find the product, copy the price, and send the link by hand. **With Shopify connected, the agent does all of that on its own**: it searches your real catalog, confirms stock, builds the pre-filled cart, and the customer pays with one click. 24/7, without anyone lifting a finger.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"People ask me about my products on WhatsApp all day. How do I make them **sell themselves** without me looking up every price and building every link?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You have a **Shopify store** with your catalog loaded | You don't use Shopify (check the other integrations) |
| You sell (or want to sell) **over WhatsApp / social** | You only sell through web checkout with no conversation |
| People message you asking about **prices, stock, products** | You don't get chat inquiries |
| You want the agent to **build the purchase link on its own** | You prefer building each sale by hand |

**Plainly put:** it's for the Shopify store that **already sells through chat** (or loses sales because it can't answer everything) and wants the AI agent to serve and close on its own.

<Aside type="note" title="Plan required: Starter (Brote) 🌱">
The Shopify integration is available **from the Starter plan**. The agent queries your catalog, verifies stock, and builds carts with no product limit.
</Aside>

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **A Shopify store** with your catalog loaded (products, variants, prices, and stock). If you already sell online, you have this.

2. **Your store's handle** — the `mitienda.myshopify.com` address or your own domain `mitienda.com`. That's all it takes to connect.

3. **A channel connected in Brote** (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger…) — that's where the customer comes in and chats with the agent.

4. An active **Starter plan** or higher.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
You don't need to know anything technical. Brote generates access to your catalog on its own, reading the public side of your store. Only if you have hidden products (private storefront) will you paste a token — we'll guide you.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Trinidad connects her Shopify in Brote** in 2 minutes: she pastes `rucatextil.cl` in Settings → Integrations → Shopify. Done — the agent now "sees" her entire real catalog.

5. **Camila pays** in the Shopify checkout. The order enters Trinidad's store like any sale — shipping address and all.

## How easy is it?

Very easy. The connection takes **2 minutes** and requires nothing technical:

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Shopify** in Brote.

2. Paste your **store's handle** (`mitienda.myshopify.com` or your own domain).

3. Brote generates access to your catalog automatically (reading the public side of your store). ✅

4. Have hidden products that don't show in the public store? Paste a Storefront token (we'll tell you where to get it) and you're done.

</Steps>

After that, **you do nothing else**. The agent already knows your catalog and starts selling in the next conversation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Brote copy my catalog?**
No. It reads your Shopify live every time a customer asks. Your prices and stock are the real ones at that moment — you're the only source of truth.

**Does the customer pay through the usual Shopify checkout?**
Yes. The agent builds a cart link that lands in your normal Shopify checkout, with your payment methods and shipping configured. If you'd rather charge through Mercado Pago inside the chat, that can be combined too.

**What about subscriptions or recurring plans?**
If your Shopify uses a subscriptions app (Recharge, Bold, Native Subscriptions), those appear as **variants** of the product. The agent detects them on its own: the customer says "I want the subscription" and the agent builds the cart with the subscription variant.

**Does the agent ask for the email before sending the link?**
It doesn't need it to sell. If the customer shares their email, the agent saves it in your CRM — but **it doesn't block the sale**: the cart link gets sent regardless, and Shopify asks for the email at checkout anyway. Less friction, more sales.

**What happens if a product is sold out?**
The agent knows before promising it. If there's no stock, it offers alternatives from your catalog or suggests a restock alert. It never sells something you don't have.

**Do I need a paid plan?**
Yes, from **Starter**. The Shopify integration isn't in the Mini plan.
