Brote connects to your **Google Calendar** directly, via Brote's own OAuth. The AI agent reads your availability and books appointments on your calendar without you having to open Google.

There are two pieces:

- **It writes the appointments** the agent books into a **"Brote — Citas"** calendar inside your Google account (kept separate and tidy).
- **It reads your availability (free/busy)** from your main calendar so it **never books over your existing events**.

It's a two-way sync: what the agent books shows up in your Google, and your personal events block those slots for the agent.

## When it matters

Verticals where scheduling is the bottleneck:

- **Healthcare and clinics**: patients requesting appointments over WhatsApp.
- **Professional services**: consultants, lawyers, dentists, stylists.
- **Real estate**: property viewings.
- **B2B**: discovery calls that don't land on Calendly.
- **Small hospitality**: custom check-in / check-out.

## How to connect

1. Go to **Settings → Scheduling**.
2. Click **"Connect Google Calendar"**.
3. Authorize with your Google account (the one with the relevant calendar).
4. Brote creates the **"Brote — Citas"** calendar for writing appointments, and you choose **which calendar(s) to read for your availability** (you can have several, e.g., "Citas Doctora Pérez", "Visitas Inmuebles Norte").

## How the agent uses it

When a customer asks for an appointment:

```
Cliente: "necesito reservar una hora con el doctor"
Agente IA:
  → invoca find_calendar_slots({calendar_id, duration: 30min, range: next_14_days})
  → recibe slots disponibles
"Tengo disponible mañana 10-11h o jueves 15-16h. Cuál te queda mejor?"

Cliente: "jueves 15h"

Agente IA:
  → invoca create_calendar_event({slot, summary, attendees: [cliente_email]})
"Confirmado, te agendé jueves 15:00. Te llega el invite a tu mail.
 Te mando recordatorio el día antes."
```

## Per-calendar settings

For each connected calendar you can define:

- **Default slot duration** (15 / 30 / 60 / 90 min).
- **Buffer** between appointments (5 / 10 / 15 min between slots).
- **Available hours** (e.g., Monday–Friday 9–13 and 15–19).
- **Minimum notice** (e.g., no appointments with less than 4 hours' notice).
- **Maximum notice** (e.g., at most 30 days into the future).

## Automatic reminders

When an appointment is booked, the agent can send automatic reminders:

- **24h before**: "Remember your appointment tomorrow at [time]".
- **1h before**: "Your appointment is in 1 hour, address X".
- **Easy reschedule**: if the customer replies "can't make it, move it", the agent proposes slots again.
