This is **the complete picture of Brote**: everything you can do, what each thing is for, who it's for and which plan it comes in. Every row links to its **"from zero to 100"** guide (with a real example, prerequisites and how it's done).

<Aside type="tip">
Just getting started? Go straight to **[Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/de-cero-a-100)** — it builds your agent for you in 15 minutes. Then come back to this map to keep adding features.
</Aside>

## 🚀 Get started (build your agent and connect your channels)

| Feature | What is it for? | Who is it for? | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/de-cero-a-100) | Builds your AI agent on its own (from a URL or audio) in 15 min | Everyone starting out | **All** |
| [AI agent](/en/empezar/agente-de-cero-a-100) | Answers your customers 24/7, qualifies, schedules and charges | Any business that serves via chat | **All** |
| [Industry templates](/en/empezar/plantillas-de-cero-a-100) | Pre-built agent for your industry, so you don't start from scratch | Businesses in common industries | **All** |
| [Channels](/en/canales/de-cero-a-100) | Where customers reach you: WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Webchat | Everyone | Webchat/WhatsApp **Mini** · IG/Messenger **Starter+** |
| [Mobile app + push](/en/empezar/app-movil-de-cero-a-100) | Respond from your phone with push alerts | Anyone not always at the PC | **All** |

## 💰 Sell (close sales inside the chat)

| Feature | What is it for? | Who is it for? | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
