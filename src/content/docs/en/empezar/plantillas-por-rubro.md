When you create your account, the onboarding asks for your **industry**. With that, Brote doesn't leave you with a blank agent: it builds one **specialized for your business** — with its personality, its conversation steps and its pipeline — and then [Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/que-es/) finishes fine-tuning it by reading your website.

There are 12 industries (clinic, real estate, ecommerce, restaurant, beauty, tourism, professional services, and more). And two of them come **ready to schedule and charge from minute zero**.

## Lodging and professional services: ready to schedule

If you pick **Tourism / Lodging** or **Professional services**, your agent arrives with [scheduling](/en/agendamiento/que-es/) already enabled **and** with a sample service loaded so you can try it right away:

- **Lodging Reception** → comes with a **"Cabaña (ejemplo)"** in [per-night stay mode](/en/agendamiento/alojamiento-por-noches/) ($45.000/night, charges upfront) with two units. The agent already knows how to quote "a cabin from the 10th to the 14th" and charge.
- **Professional Booking** → comes with a chargeable 30-min **"Consulta (ejemplo)"**, with one professional and a Monday-to-Friday schedule. The agent can already offer a slot and charge for it.

:::tip[Just edit in your real data]
The sample services exist so you can see how it works. Go to **Settings → Scheduling** and swap them for your real data (price, hours, units) — or delete them and create your own. Nothing breaks.
:::

## How do I try it?

As soon as onboarding finishes, message your agent from the [test webchat](/en/canales/webchat/) or via WhatsApp:

- Lodging: *"do you have availability from the 10th to the 14th?"* → the agent quotes the nights and sends you the payment link.
- Professional: *"I want to book an appointment"* → the agent offers a real time slot and, if the service charges, sends you the link.

## The other industries

The rest of the industries also generate a specialized agent (persona, FAQs, steps, pipeline), ready to handle sales and support. If your business books by the hour or by the night, you can enable scheduling whenever you want by following [Configure services and hours](/en/agendamiento/configurar/).

:::note[Plan]
Scheduling and in-chat payments are available from the **Starter** plan.
:::
