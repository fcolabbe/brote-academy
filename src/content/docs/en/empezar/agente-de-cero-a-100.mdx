## In one sentence

**AI agent** = an employee that serves your customers **24/7 on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger**, answers their questions, qualifies who's a good lead, books appointments and even charges them — all in your business's tone and without you glued to your phone.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"Messages come in at all hours and I can't keep up. What if someone answered **for me**, well, always, and only passed along what matters?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| Customers message you on **WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger** and you can't answer them all | You don't get customer messages (100% in-person sales, no chat) |
| You always answer **the same things** (prices, hours, stock, how to buy) | Every inquiry is unique and needs expert human judgment each time |
| You lose sales because **you reply late** (at night, weekends, while working) | You already have a team answering instantly 24/7 with capacity to spare |
| You want to **qualify, schedule or charge** without doing it by hand every time | You only want a manual chat with no automation at all |

**In plain terms:** it's for any business that **serves customers via chat** and wants to reply fast, always, without cloning themselves.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: ALL (it's the heart of Brote)">
The AI agent **is not an add-on** — it's the heart of Brote and available on **all plans**, starting with the most basic (Mini). What changes between plans is **volume** (how many messages/conversations) and the **advanced features** around the agent (more channels, scheduling, sales attribution, etc.). But the agent that answers and sells: you have it from day 1, on any plan.
</Aside>

## What do you need before?

<Steps>

1. **A Brote account** — sign up free at [brote.ai](https://brote.ai/es) and test the agent in simulation mode (Playground) before exposing it to real customers.

2. **A connected channel** — at least **one**: WhatsApp (the most used), Instagram or Messenger. It's the door customers come in through and where the agent replies. WhatsApp connects by scanning a QR, like WhatsApp Web.

3. **Your business info** — so the agent knows what to sell and how. You have **two paths** (pick one or combine both):
   - **Your site's URL** — Brote Copilot reads it on its own and builds the agent.
   - **A 2-5 minute audio** — you tell it, like briefing a new employee, what you sell, prices and rules.

4. **Nothing technical.** You don't write prompts, don't code, don't hire a consultant. **Copilot** does all the configuration work for you.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
No website, or a very basic one? Perfect: record a 2-5 minute audio and done. It's the fastest path when your website has no prices or descriptions.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Diego ☕

> **Diego** owns **"Café Nube"**, a specialty coffee shop in Valparaíso that also sells beans and takes orders via WhatsApp. He gets 40-50 messages a day and replies when he can — often the next day, by which time the customer is gone.

<Steps>

1. **Diego signs up for Brote** on a Tuesday afternoon. Instead of writing a giant prompt, he pastes the URL of `cafenube.cl`.

2. **Brote Copilot reads his site** — it extracts the coffees he sells, the prices ($8.900 per kilo of beans), the hours, the delivery area — and in a few minutes builds the agent: a warm, local tone, a welcome message and the frequent questions already answered. *(Since Diego's site didn't have the shipping policy, he also records a 3-minute audio explaining it. Copilot adds it in.)*

3. **Diego tests the agent in the Playground** (simulation mode, only he sees it). He asks things like "do you ship to Viña?" and the agent answers well. He tweaks a couple of details and gives it the OK. **Total: 15 minutes.**

4. **He connects his WhatsApp** by scanning a QR. The agent is **live, serving 24/7**.

5. **That same night, at 11:40 PM**, Camila writes: "Hi! Do you have decaf beans?". The agent replies instantly: yes, shows her the decaf, the price, and asks whether she wants delivery or pickup. Camila orders 1 kilo.

6. **The agent sends her a payment link** in the chat. Camila pays. The agent confirms the order and books her pickup for the next day at 11:00. **Diego was asleep.**

</Steps>

**A week later, Diego opens Brote and sees:**

| Before (by hand) | With the AI agent |
|---|---|
| Replied in ~6 hours (or the next day) | Replies in **seconds, at any hour** |
| Lost sales at night and on weekends | Closed **11 after-hours orders** 🌙 |
| Answered 40 repetitive messages a day | The agent filtered everything; only **3 cases** that truly needed a human reached Diego |
| Zero follow-up | The agent **qualified** who was a buyer and who was just browsing |

**Diego's (obvious) decision:** *"Now I make the coffee and the agent sells. I only look at the 2-3 messages that actually need me."* That's going from **zero to 100**: from losing sales by replying late, to selling while you sleep.

<Aside type="tip" title="And it improves on its own">
The agent doesn't stay the same. **Brote Copilot Audit** reads the real conversations, detects where it answered weakly and proposes improvements you accept or reject. In 30 days your agent answers better than on day 1 — without you touching anything.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Do customers notice they're talking to an AI?**
The agent replies naturally, in your business's tone. You decide how "assistant-like" it sounds. And when a case calls for it, it **hands off to a human** on your team automatically.

**What if the agent doesn't know something?**
You define what to do in those cases (the *edge cases*): it can ask for more details, give a safe answer, or **pass the conversation to a person**. It never makes wild things up if you configure it right — and Copilot already builds those cases for you.

**Can I change how it replies?**
Yes, whenever you want. You edit its tone, its answers and its rules from the dashboard. And if you update your site or your business, **re-run Copilot** in one click and it catches up.

**Which channels does it work on?**
WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger (among others). You can have **one or several at once** — the agent handles all of them with the same brain.

**Do I need the most expensive plan?**
No. The AI agent is in **all plans starting with Mini**. Higher plans add more message volume and features around it (more channels, scheduling, sales attribution), but the agent that sells is yours from the most basic plan.

**Can I try it without signing up?**
Yes. In the [public demo](https://brote.ai/es/demo) you paste any URL and see the generated agent in ~60 seconds, without creating an account.
