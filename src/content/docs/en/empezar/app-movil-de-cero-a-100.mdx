## In one sentence

The **Brote mobile app** is your business in your pocket: install Brote on your phone and get a **push notification** the moment a new message or lead comes in — so you can reply from wherever you are, without being stuck at the computer.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"I'm out of the office. How do I find out instantly that a customer messaged me and reply without losing the sale?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You run your business yourself or with a small team | You never leave the computer |
| You're out in the field, in meetings, delivering, or simply live on your phone | You have a big office-only team with screens always open |
| You lose sales because you reply to messages **late** | You already reply instantly from the browser and don't need push |
| You want to know **instantly** when a lead comes in or the AI agent hands off a case | You're not interested in getting alerts on your phone |

**In plain terms:** it's for the owner or salesperson who **doesn't live in front of the computer** and doesn't want to lose a customer by replying late.

<Aside type="note" title="Plan required: all">
The mobile app and push notifications are available on **all plans** (Mini, Starter, Pro and Business). It's not a premium feature — it's part of how you use Brote every day.
</Aside>

## What do you need before?

<Steps>

1. **An active Brote account.** If you don't have one yet, [create your account](/en/empezar/crear-cuenta/) first (it takes 2 minutes).

2. **At least one connected channel** (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Webchat or Email). That's where the messages the push alerts you about will come from. If you haven't connected anything yet, start by [connecting WhatsApp](/en/empezar/conectar-whatsapp/).

3. **Your phone** with an internet connection. Nothing else: no odd installs, nothing technical to know.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
The app runs on your same Brote account. Nothing new to create, no data to migrate: you log in with the same email and password you use in the browser, and you see exactly your same Inbox, contacts and agent.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Rodrigo 🌱

> **Rodrigo** owns **"Ferretería El Roble"**, a hardware store with home delivery in Coquimbo. He handles WhatsApp on his own, but spends half the day in the warehouse, in the truck delivering, or buying stock. He used to lose sales because he only saw messages at night, back at the computer.

<Steps>

1. **Rodrigo installs the Brote app** on his phone and logs in with his usual email and password. The app asks if he wants notifications and he taps **"Allow"**. Done — set up in under 2 minutes.

2. **It's mid-morning and Rodrigo is out delivering in the truck.** A new customer messages him on WhatsApp: "Hi, do you have a hammer drill? Do you deliver to La Serena today?".

3. **Brote's AI agent replies on its own** instantly: confirms they have it, the price, and that they deliver same-day. But the customer asks something specific about the warranty → the agent **hands the case off to a human**.

4. **At that instant the push goes off on Rodrigo's phone:** *"🔔 A customer needs your help — Ferretería El Roble"*. Rodrigo pulls over, opens the app with one tap and **is already in the conversation**, with all the context the agent gathered.

5. **Rodrigo replies in 30 seconds** from the sidewalk: "Yes, the drill comes with a 2-year warranty. Here's the payment link". He closes the sale without going back to the office.

6. **That same week** 3 more leads come in while he's in the field. He answers all 3 the moment the push sounds. Before, those 3 would have gone cold until nighttime.

</Steps>

**At the end of the month, Rodrigo does the math:**

| Before (no app) | After (with app + push) |
|---|---|
| Saw field messages only at night | Finds out instantly via push |
| ~2 out of 10 leads went cold from late replies | Answers almost all within minutes |
| Had to go back to the office to respond properly | Closes sales from the truck |

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
You stop losing sales to late replies. The push alerts you **in the moment**, and you reply from wherever you are. A lead answered in 2 minutes is worth far more than one answered in 6 hours.
</Aside>

## What will you achieve?

- **Find out instantly** when a new message or lead comes in, or the AI agent hands off a case.
- **Reply from your phone** with the same Inbox, contacts and agent you use in the browser.
- **Stop losing sales** to delays: the customer who gets a fast reply is the one who buys.
- **Carry your business in your pocket** — respond from the street, a meeting or your couch.

<Aside type="note" title="Which alerts can you get?">
You choose what notifies you: when the agent **hands off a case** to a human (handoff), when a **new lead** comes in, or when there are **unread messages**. You adjust this from your account settings so you don't get too many.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. Install the app, log in with your usual account and tap **"Allow"** when it asks about notifications. There's nothing to configure per message or per channel: once you've granted permission, push works on its own forever.

<Aside type="caution" title="Important: tap 'Allow' on notifications">
If you accidentally tap "Don't allow" when the app asks, you won't receive alerts. You can turn them back on from your phone's settings (Settings → Notifications → Brote AI → Allow).
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it a "real" native app or the website inside an app?**
It's the Brote website inside an app. You see exactly the same Brote as in the browser, but packaged as an app so **push notifications work reliably** — especially on iPhone, where browser alerts are unreliable. The best of both worlds: the app you know, with push that actually arrives.

**Which stores is the app in?**
The **iPhone (iOS)** app is in **Apple's final review, coming to the App Store very soon** (search for **"Brote AI"**). The **Android** one is in the final testing stage before publishing on Google Play. In both cases, if you want in earlier, message us and we'll add you to the early-access list. In the meantime, Brote works perfectly from your phone's browser (and you can "add it to your home screen" to have it as an app).

**Do I need to pay extra for the app?**
No. The app and notifications are included in all plans. Same account, no additional cost.

**Can my whole team use the app?**
Yes. Each person on your team installs the app, logs in with their own user and gets their own alerts on their phone.

**If I already use Brote in my phone's browser, why install the app?**
For the push. In the browser (especially on iPhone) notifications are unreliable or simply don't arrive. With the app installed, the alert reaches you instantly even with the app closed.

**Are the messages the same ones I see on the computer?**
Yes, it's your same account. What you reply from your phone appears on the computer and vice versa. It's one single Brote across all your devices.
