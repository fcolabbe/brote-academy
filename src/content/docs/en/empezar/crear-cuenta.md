Creating an account takes 2 minutes. You don't need a credit card.

![Brote's public pricing with the 4 official plans](/screenshots/pricing-plans.png)

## Steps

1. Go to **[brote.ai/signup](https://brote.ai/es/signup)** (you can also click any _"Empieza gratis · 14 días"_ button on the site).
2. Enter your **email and password** (or use Google if you prefer).
3. Confirm your email by clicking the link you receive.
4. Done: your account is active with a **14-day trial on the Starter plan**.

## What the trial includes

During the 14 days you get full access to the Starter plan:

- **500 AI conversations / month**
- **WhatsApp + Webchat** active
- **Shopify and Mercado Pago integrations**
- **Kanban pipeline + tags + notes**
- **2 team members**

Full details in [14-day trial](/en/planes/trial/).

## What happens on day 14?

- If you **add a card + choose a plan** → you continue normally on the chosen plan.
- If you do **NOT add a card** → your account switches to **read-only mode for 30 days** (you can view conversations / contacts / settings, but the AI agent is paused). After 30 days without payment, the account is archived and the data is preserved for 180 days.

## Billing

We work with **Polar as Merchant of Record**:

- Polar charges in your local currency (CLP, MXN, ARS, COP, USD…)
- Handles VAT per country automatically
- Issues your invoice

Brote never touches the money. You just pick the plan.

## Regional coupon

If your SMB is in the **Coquimbo Region** (Chile), you can apply the **COQUIMBO coupon**, which brings the Starter plan down to **USD $23/month for 6 months** (instead of $49). URL with the discount auto-applied:

[brote.ai/es/pricing?coupon=COQUIMBO](https://brote.ai/es/pricing?coupon=COQUIMBO)
