## In one sentence

**Industry templates** = your AI agent arrives **pre-built for your type of business**, instead of leaving you facing a blank page.

When you create your account, the onboarding asks you one key thing: **your industry**. With that answer, Brote builds a **specialized** agent — with its personality, its conversation steps, its sales pipeline and its tags — designed around how a business like yours sells and serves. In **minutes** you have an agent that already knows how to answer, and in two industries it even knows how to **schedule and charge**.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"I just opened my account. Do I have to configure everything from scratch, or does my agent already come knowing how my industry works?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | You don't need to think about it if… |
|---|---|
| You're **just starting** with Brote and don't want to build the agent from scratch | You already have a fine-tuned agent up and running |
| Your business fits a **typical industry** (retail, healthcare, lodging, services, real estate…) | Your operation is so unique you'd rather start blank (you still can) |
| You want to **start today** and fine-tune details later | You need every detail perfect before the first conversation |

**In plain terms:** it's for whoever opens an account and wants to **be answering the same day**, not after a week of configuration.

<Aside type="note" title="Which plan do I need?">
Industry templates come with **all plans** — even Mini (Semilla). What does require the **Starter (Brote)** plan is the **scheduling and in-chat payments** part, which is exactly what the **Lodging** and **Professional Booking** templates ship ready. With Mini you get the specialized agent; from Starter up, it also schedules and charges.
</Aside>

## What do you need before?

<Steps>

1. **A Brote account** (the onboarding walks you through it). In the first step you pick your **industry** from a list.

2. **Nothing technical.** No prompts to write, no steps to build, no AI knowledge required. You just pick your industry and Brote does the rest.

3. **(Optional but recommended) Your website or social media URL.** That way [Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/que-es/) reads your real business and **fine-tunes the agent's personality** for you — on top of the base template.

4. **(Only if you want to schedule/charge)** **Starter** plan or higher. The Lodging and Professional Booking industries ship with it turned on.

</Steps>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Rodrigo 🌱

> **Rodrigo** just opened **"Cabañas Bosquemar"**, three cabins rented by the night in southern Chile. He's never configured a chatbot in his life. He sits down on a Tuesday afternoon to create his Brote account.

<Steps>

1. **Rodrigo creates his account.** The onboarding asks for his industry. He picks **Tourism / Lodging**.

2. **In seconds, his agent already exists.** It's not a blank chatbot: it's a **"Lodging Reception"** with a host's personality, the steps for taking a reservation, a sales pipeline set up — and a **"Cabaña (ejemplo)"** at $45.000/night already loaded, ready to quote and charge.

3. **Rodrigo tries it right away.** From the [test webchat](/en/canales/webchat/) he writes: *"do you have availability from the 10th to the 14th?"*. The agent quotes the 4 nights ($180.000), asks for his details and sends him a **payment link**. All without Rodrigo touching a single setting.

4. **He pastes his site's URL into [Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/que-es/).** Copilot reads "Cabañas Bosquemar", understands there are hot tubs, pets allowed and check-in at 3:00 PM, and **fine-tunes the agent's personality** with those real details.

5. **Rodrigo swaps the example for his own.** He goes to **Settings → Scheduling**, replaces the "Cabaña (ejemplo)" with his 3 real cabins, their prices and photos, and deletes the example. 15 minutes.

6. **He connects his WhatsApp** and that same afternoon his number is already answering availability, booking and charging. **Zero manual agent configuration.**

</Steps>

**What it would have taken Rodrigo to build from scratch** (writing the personality, defining every conversation step, setting up the pipeline, figuring out how nights are charged) **took him 0 minutes**: the template already had it. He only changed the data that's his.

You don't start from a blank page. You start from an agent that **already knows how your industry works** and you only change what's yours. It's the difference between answering **today** or a week from now.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I edit the template?**
Yes, everything. The template is a **starting point**, not a cage. You change the personality, the steps, the pipeline, the services, the prices — whatever you want. [Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/que-es/) helps you fine-tune the personality by reading your website, and you adjust the rest by hand from the agent editor.

**What if my industry isn't on the list?**
Pick the **closest one** or the **Generic** industry (a flexible base that handles sales and support). From there you adapt the agent to your case with Copilot and the editor. You'll still start ahead: never from a blank page.

**Do the sample services actually charge money?**
Not until you want them to. The "Cabaña (ejemplo)" and the "Consulta (ejemplo)" exist so you can see **how** the agent quotes and charges. Swap them for your real data or delete them in **Settings → Scheduling**.

**Picked the wrong industry — do I have to start over?**
No. You can keep editing the agent you have, or enable [scheduling](/en/agendamiento/configurar/) and any tools you're missing whenever you want. The template doesn't chain you to anything.

**Do I need a paid plan to get a template?**
No. All plans come with the industry-specialized agent. Only the **scheduling and in-chat payments** part (which Lodging and Professional Booking ship enabled) requires the **Starter** plan or higher.
