This is the magic part. Brote Copilot configures the full AI agent in 15 minutes from **your website** or **a 2-5 minute audio** where you explain how your business operates.

![Public demo at brote.ai/es/demo where you try the agent without signing up](/screenshots/demo-empty.png)

## Option 1 — Paste your site's URL (recommended)

1. In the dashboard, go to **Onboarding** or **AI Agent → Regenerate**.
2. Paste your site's full URL (e.g. `https://misitio.cl`).
3. Brote Copilot:
   - Reads your home page + internal pages (Contact, About, Services)
   - Extracts products, prices, services, contact info, hours
   - Detects your vertical
   - Synthesizes the **agent's persona** (name, tone, opening message)
   - Configures **frequent FAQs** with answers
   - Defines **edge cases** (what to do when it doesn't know, when to hand off to a human)

In **5-15 minutes** your agent is ready to reply.

## Option 2 — Record an audio

If your website is basic, a text-light Wix kind of site, or you don't have one:

1. In the onboarding wizard, choose **"Tell Copilot by audio"**.
2. Record **2-5 minutes** explaining:
   - What you sell / what service you offer
   - Who your customers are
   - Typical prices
   - Business rules (payment methods, warranties, hours)
   - The tone you want the agent to use
3. Brote Copilot transcribes + classifies + builds the agent.

## Refinement

After the first generation, you can:

- **Edit blocks** of the prompt (persona, instruction, edge cases) in the dashboard.
- **Re-run Copilot** if you updated the site.
- Enable **[Brote Copilot Audit](/en/copilot/audit/)**, which detects gaps in real answers and proposes quick-tunes.

![AI agent configuration in the Brote dashboard](/screenshots/agent-config.png)

## Test before connecting WhatsApp

Brote includes a **Playground** where you can chat with the agent in simulation before exposing it to real customers. It's at **AI Agent → Playground** (admins only).
