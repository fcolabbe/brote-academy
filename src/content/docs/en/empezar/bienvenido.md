Brote is a **conversational AI agent** that serves your customers on WhatsApp, Webchat, Instagram, Messenger and Email, charges with Mercado Pago inside the chat and hands off to a human when needed.

![Brote dashboard — real-time weekly KPIs](/screenshots/dashboard-home.png)

Unlike a traditional chatbot:

- **You don't write prompts**: the agent configures itself from your website or a 2-5 minute audio where you explain how your business operates.
- **No consultant required**: you go from the audio to the agent replying on WhatsApp in 15 minutes.
- **It's not just support**: it sells, schedules, quotes and charges.
