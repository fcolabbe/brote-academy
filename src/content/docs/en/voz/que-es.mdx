Your Brote agent can reply to the customer with **a voice note that sounds
exactly like you** — not like a robot voice, not like a voice actor. This is
what we call **cloned AI Voice**.

## How it works

1. Once, you record a 30-45 second sample reading naturally.
2. Brote sends that sample to ElevenLabs (the cloning provider), which
   generates a unique `voice_id` for your business.
3. From then on, any text you write or that the AI agent wants to reply
   with can be turned into audio with your voice, inside the chat.

<Aside type="tip">
The voice is stored on your organization — all team members send audios
with that same voice. If your business has several "characters", we can
add multi-voice support; just ask.
</Aside>

## Set up your voice

1. Go to **Settings → AI Voice** (`/app/settings/voice`).
2. Click **Start recording** and read the suggested text (any paragraph
   about your business works).
3. Minimum 20 seconds, ideally 45. Stop whenever you want.
4. Click **Clone this voice** → 5-15 sec wait.
5. Once cloned, you can:
   - Try it with any text from the "Test the voice" block.
   - Retrain with a new recording.
   - Delete it and redo it from scratch.

## Use the voice in a conversation

### From a quick reply (`/` shortcut)

If in `/app/settings/quick-replies` you created a shortcut in **Audio (TTS)
mode**, typing `/saludo` makes the agent generate audio with your voice,
resolve the `{{contact.first_name}}` variables and send it straight to the
customer on WhatsApp as a voice note.

### Ad-hoc from the composer

Coming soon: a TTS button in the composer to convert any text into audio
before sending it. For now use quick replies.

## Caps per plan

Each plan has a monthly cap of generated characters (not audios — chars,
because ElevenLabs charges per character):

| Plan | Included / mo | Hard cap | Overage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial | 500 | 500 | — |
| Mini ($29) | 0 | 0 | feature locked |
| Starter ($49) | 5,000 | 15,000 | $0.20 / 100 chars |
| Pro ($129) | 30,000 | 75,000 | $0.18 / 100 chars |
| Business ($349) | 150,000 | 350,000 | $0.15 / 100 chars |
| Enterprise | unlimited | unlimited | — |

- **Included**: free within the plan.
- **Overage**: beyond what's included, it keeps working but will be
  charged at the end of the month in an upcoming Brote update (Polar).
  Today it's informational.
- **Hard cap**: beyond this, Brote blocks generation and shows the
  exceeded cap. You can generate again next month or upgrade your plan.

Live usage is shown at `/app/settings/voice` with progress bars —
yellow (overage zone) and red (hard cap reached).

## Common errors

**`paid_plan_required` when cloning**: your ElevenLabs account is Free.
It needs the Starter plan ($5/mo) or higher to use Instant Voice Cloning.
Brote doesn't control this — the upgrade is done directly at elevenlabs.io.

**`hard_cap_exceeded` when sending audio**: you exceeded the absolute
monthly cap. Wait for the next period or ask us for a Brote plan upgrade.

**Voice sounds off**: the cloning sample was low quality (background
noise, echo, bad microphone). Retrain with a clean recording.
