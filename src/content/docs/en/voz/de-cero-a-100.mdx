## In one sentence

**Cloned voice** = your AI agent replies to the customer with a **voice note that actually sounds like you** on WhatsApp — not a robot voice, not a voice actor: **your voice**.

Brote clones your voice once (from a 30-45 second sample). Afterwards, when the moment calls for it, the AI agent replies in audio with that voice: a greeting, a thank-you, a warm confirmation. In a channel where everyone writes cold text, **a human voice note builds trust and closes more**.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"My customers close when they hear my voice. Can my AI agent reply in audio **as if it were me**, without me being there?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You sell **trust and closeness** — services, healthcare, real estate, coaching, consulting | You sell products where the price/spec closes on its own |
| Your customers send you **voice notes** and expect similar replies | Your audience prefers text and links only |
| You serve customers on **WhatsApp** (the only channel with voice for now) | You sell by email or web chat |
| **You are the face** of the business (broker, doctor, coach, advisor) | You're a brand without a personal face/voice |

**In plain terms:** it's for the business where **a human voice closes more than text** — the one selling a relationship, not just a catalog.

<Aside type="caution" title="Required plan: Starter (Brote) or higher">
Cloned voice starts on the **Starter** plan. Each plan includes a monthly audio allowance (measured in characters, because the voice provider charges that way). The **Mini** plan doesn't include voice. The exact table of what each plan includes is below.
</Aside>

## What do you need before starting?

<Steps>

1. **WhatsApp connected in Brote** — it's the only channel where the agent sends audio today.

2. **An active Starter, Pro or Business plan** (Mini doesn't include voice).

3. **A 30-45 second sample of your voice**: a quiet spot, reading any paragraph about your business naturally. That's all Brote needs to clone your voice.

4. **Your AI agent built** — the voice is how it replies; first you need the what (your agent replying in text), then you add the how (audio).

5. (Optional) The `send_voice_reply` function **enabled** on your agent, so the LLM decides on its own when to send audio.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="What if my voice sounds bad in the sample?">
Clone quality depends on the sample. Record in a quiet place, no echo, with the microphone close. If the result sounds off, retrain with a cleaner recording — you can redo it as many times as you want.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Rodrigo 🌱

> **Rodrigo** is an **independent real estate broker** in La Serena. He works alone, handles dozens of WhatsApp inquiries a day and knows one thing: *when the customer hears his voice, they trust him and book the visit*. The problem is he can't keep up recording an audio for each one.

<Steps>

1. **Rodrigo clones his voice once.** He goes to Brote → Settings → AI Voice, records 40 seconds reading an apartment description and taps **Clone**. In 10 seconds his voice is ready.

2. **He enables `send_voice_reply` on his agent** and adds to the prompt: *"When the customer greets or thanks, reply with audio in my voice; for prices and details, use text."*

3. **Camila writes on a Saturday at 9 PM:** "Hi, I saw the apartment on Av. del Mar, is it still available? 😍". Rodrigo is at a barbecue, not looking at his phone.

4. **The AI agent replies in audio, with Rodrigo's voice:** *"Hi Camila! Yes, it's still available, so glad you liked it 🙌 Let me send you the details."* Camila hears the voice note and feels **the broker himself** answered her, on a Saturday night.

5. **The agent continues in text** with the price, common expenses and a link to schedule the visit. Camila books for Tuesday.

6. **Rodrigo only sees the conversation on Sunday** — with the visit already booked. The customer never knew an AI replied.

</Steps>

**After a month using cloned voice, Rodrigo compares:**

| Metric | Before (text only) | With cloned voice |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiries that book a visit | 12 out of 100 | 19 out of 100 |
| Replies outside business hours | 0 (answered the next day) | Instant, in his voice |
| Time recording audios by hand | 40 min/day | 0 min |

**Translated:** out of every 100 inquiries, he went from **12 to 19 booked visits** — 58% more — without working more hours. The voice he used to record by hand is now sent by his agent, sounding exactly like him.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you achieve">
Your closeness stops depending on your availability. The agent replies 24/7 **with your voice** → the customer feels it's you answering → they trust and close. You sell your relationship **on autopilot**.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. The "hard" part is recording 40 clean seconds, once.

<Steps>

1. **Record your voice** (30-45 sec) in Settings → AI Voice and tap Clone. Under 1 minute.

2. **Enable the audio function** on your agent. One click.

3. **Tell the agent when to use voice** in its prompt (or keep the default suggestion). Done.

</Steps>

From there it runs on its own: the agent decides when to send audio and when text. And if you run out of your monthly audio allowance, it **falls back to text automatically** — it never leaves the customer without a reply.

<Aside type="note" title="How long until it's ready?">
Cloning the voice: 5-15 seconds. Setting everything up: under 5 minutes. You don't need to know anything technical.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it my real voice or an imitation?**
It's **your real voice**, cloned from the sample you record. It's not an actor or a generic catalog voice — it's Brote cloning your voice with the cloning provider (ElevenLabs). No other platform in LatAm offers an AI agent with the voice **cloned from the business itself**.

**Does it sound robotic?**
No, if the sample is good. The clone copies your tone, your rhythm and your accent. The key is recording clean (no noise or echo). If it sounded off, retrain with a better recording and it comes out natural.

**How much audio does my plan include?**
Starter: 5,000 characters/mo. Pro: 30,000. Business: 150,000. Mini doesn't include voice. As a reference, a greeting audio is ~100-200 characters, so Starter covers quite a few short notes per month. The full table is above.

**Which channels does it work on?**
Only **WhatsApp** for now. It's where the customer expects and values a voice note. Other channels go by text.

**What happens if I run out of allowance?**
The agent **falls back to text automatically** — the customer always gets a reply, just written instead of audio. You get voice back the next month or by upgrading.

**Does the customer realize it's an AI?**
No. They hear a voice note that sounds like you and feel you replied in person.
