## In one sentence

**Mass campaign** = sending **the same WhatsApp message to hundreds of contacts at once** (a promo, an announcement, a reminder), with the **AI agent continuing each conversation** based on what each person replies.

You already have a customer base: people who bought from you, leads that went lukewarm, subscribers who cooled off. That base is money sitting idle. A campaign wakes it up: you pick who, write the message, and Brote sends it while respecting limits so you **don't burn your WhatsApp**. What sets it apart from a traditional "blast": you're not left with 500 unanswered replies — the AI agent responds, recommends, builds the payment link and labels each contact by their real intent.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"I have 500 customers in my contact list. How do I tell them about my end-of-season promo **without writing one by one** and without Meta banning my number?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You have a loaded **contact base** (customers, leads) you want to reactivate | You're just starting and barely have contacts |
| You want to announce **promos, news or reminders** to many at once | You only handle inbound (pure 1:1) |
| You sell over **WhatsApp** and want the conversation to continue on its own | You don't sell through conversation |
| You're on the **Pro or Business** plan | You're on Mini or Starter (they don't include campaigns) |

**Plainly put:** it's for the business that already has **a list of people** and wants to sell to them again — with no marketing team and without risking the number.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: Pro or Business — with caps that protect you">
Mass campaigns are a **Pro and Business** feature. Mini and Starter are designed for 1:1 conversations (handling what comes in), not mass sends. Each plan has **daily and monthly limits that Brote enforces automatically** so Meta doesn't throttle or ban your number:

| Plan | Campaigns/month | Contacts/campaign | Messages/day (total) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Mini** ($29) | 0 — no campaigns | — | — |
| **Starter** ($49) | 0 — no campaigns | — | — |
| **Pro** ($129) | 10 | 500 | 800 |
| **Business** ($349) | unlimited | 1.500 | 2.500 |

These numbers are calibrated with real WhatsApp tolerance data. They're not arbitrary: exceeding them is how you earn a ban. Details in [Caps per plan](/en/campanas/caps/).
</Aside>

## What do you need before launching?

<Steps>

1. **Contacts loaded in Brote.** A campaign goes out to a *cohort* — a filtered group of your base. If your list is empty, first load or import contacts (via CSV or the ones who already chatted with your agent).

2. **WhatsApp connected.** The channel campaigns go out through. The recommended path is Meta's **official Cloud API** (better number reputation); the standard connection also works.

3. **Respect opt-in / opt-out.** Send only to people who **gave you permission or already chatted with you** — not purchased number lists. And don't worry about opt-out: if someone replies "stop" or "unsubscribe", **Brote removes them automatically** from all future campaigns (details below).

4. An active **Pro or Business plan**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
You don't need any technical knowledge. If you have contacts, WhatsApp connected and a Pro+ plan, you're ready to launch. The wizard is 4 steps (segment → message → goal → schedule). See [Create your first campaign](/en/campanas/crear/).
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Camila 🌱

> **Camila** runs **"Trébol"**, a clothing store that sells over WhatsApp and Instagram. Winter season is ending and she's stuck with stock. She has **520 customers** in Brote who bought at some point. She wants to clear the warehouse with a **30% end-of-season sale** — but she knows writing to 520 people by hand is impossible, and that a reckless blast could cost her the number.

<Steps>

1. **Camila segments (Step 1).** Instead of sending to all 520, she filters tightly: customers with a **purchase in the last 12 months** + the `invierno` tag. Brote shows her live: **"487 contacts match"**. Since she's on **Pro** (500 contacts/campaign cap), it just fits. She trims a bit more to the most active and lands on **450**.

2. **Camila writes the message (Step 2).** Something human, not robotic:
   *"Hi `{nombre}` 👋 We're closing out winter with 30% off everything you liked. Here's the link, it runs through Sunday."* + a photo of the sale rack + a **pre-built payment link**. The `{nombre}` variable is replaced automatically in each send.

3. **Camila sets the goal (Step 3):** *Payment*. That way Brote tracks how many of those 450 people actually end up buying, not just how many reply.

4. **Camila schedules the send (Step 4).** She picks "tomorrow 10:00". Her Pro plan has an **800 messages/day** cap, so all 450 go out **the same day** with no queuing. Brote also respects each contact's **local waking hours** (no 3 AM messages).

5. **The next day, the campaign runs on its own.** Of the 450:
   - 40 reply "no thanks / not for me" → the AI agent closes politely. **3 write "unsubscribe"** → Brote marks them `opted_out` and they never receive campaigns again.
   - 90 ask about sizes, colors, "do you have the blue coat?" → the AI agent answers from the catalog, without Camila touching anything.
   - Interested contacts get the **payment link** from the agent and buy.

6. **Camila opens the dashboard and sees the full funnel:** sent → delivered → replied → **paid**.

</Steps>

**Camila's campaign results (one afternoon, zero messages written by hand):**

| Metric | Number |
|---|---|
| Contacts in the cohort | 450 |
| Replied | 130 (29%) |
| **Sales closed** | **38 · ~$1.140.000** 🎉 |
| Opt-outs | 3 (0.7% — healthy) |
| WhatsApp bans | 0 (Brote enforced the cap) |

**Camila's takeaway (in 10 seconds):** *"38 sales from a rack I was going to liquidate at a loss. Next season I'll run the campaign before clearing out."* Without campaigns she would have dumped that stock at half price in the physical store — or not sold it at all.

You turn your customer list into **repeat sales** — no marketing team, no writing one by one, and no risking your number. The base you already have is your cheapest sales channel: reactivating it costs next to nothing compared to acquiring a new customer.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can my WhatsApp get banned for sending campaigns?**
That's exactly the risk the **caps** and **opt-out** exist to prevent. Meta detects mass-send patterns and can throttle or ban a number that overdoes it. That's why each plan has a daily/monthly ceiling that **Brote enforces for you** — it never lets you over-send. Add to that sending only to people who gave you permission and respecting anyone who asks out, and you're within tolerable bounds. Recovering a banned number means 1 to 7 days of downtime, so the cap is deliberately calibrated conservative.

**What is automatic opt-out?**
If a contact replies "stop", "unsubscribe", "no more messages" or variants, the AI agent **closes politely**, applies the `opted_out` tag and **excludes them from all future campaigns** — even if one of your filters technically includes them, Brote leaves them out. No manual unsubscribe lists. If a campaign generates **more than 3% opt-out**, Brote alerts you to review the message (it usually means the cohort was too aggressive or it read like spam). Details in [Automatic opt-out](/en/campanas/opt-out/).

**Should I use WhatsApp via the official Cloud API or the standard connection?**
Meta's **official Cloud API** is the recommended one: your number gets better reputation and sends are more stable. The standard connection also works, but it's more sensitive to mass-send patterns — which is why caps are the key protection either way. In practice, **the vast majority of Brote campaigns go out over WhatsApp** because that's where your base is. For Instagram/Messenger there are no Brote-specific caps, but those platforms heavily restrict mass sends to non-followers.

**How many contacts can I send per campaign?**
Depends on the plan: **Pro** up to **500** per campaign and **800/day** total; **Business** up to **1.500** per campaign and **2.500/day**. If you have more contacts than the daily cap, Brote **queues** the rest for the following days automatically. See [Caps per plan](/en/campanas/caps/).

**Can the same person receive two campaigns?**
Yes, unless they've opted out. For cap counting, a contact who's in 2 cohorts counts twice.
