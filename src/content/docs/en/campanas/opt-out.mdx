When a contact replies to a campaign asking not to receive more messages, Brote detects it automatically and **excludes them from all future campaigns**. No unsubscribe lists to build, no manual filtering.

## Words that trigger opt-out

The AI agent recognizes Spanish variants of unsubscribe requests:

- "stop", "STOP", "baja", "darme de baja"
- "no quiero más mensajes", "no me mandes más", "déjame en paz"
- "unsubscribe", "cancelar suscripción"
- "borrar", "elimíname de tu lista"

It also recognizes the English/Portuguese equivalents if your agent is configured as bilingual.

## What happens on opt-out

1. **It closes the conversation politely**: "Done, I'm taking you off the list. If you ever want to receive info again, message me and I'll reactivate you."
2. **It marks the contact** with the `opted_out` tag (visible on their profile).
3. **It excludes them** from future cohorts — even if you build filters that technically include them, Brote leaves them out.
4. **If you have Webhooks/Zapier enabled**, it fires a `contact_opted_out` event so your CRM/external systems mark them too.

<Aside type="tip" title="It doesn't block inbound conversations">
Opt-out applies to **outbound mass campaigns**. If that same contact messages you first later ("hi, I have a question"), the AI agent replies normally — that's not spam, it's support. The only thing blocked is your system initiating a message to them.
</Aside>

## LATAM compliance

Automatic opt-out keeps you compliant with:

- **LGPD (Brazil)**: right to withdraw consent.
- **LFPDPPP (Mexico)**: right to object.
- **Law 19.628 (Chile)**: cancellation of data processing.
- **Law 25.326 (Argentina)**: right to deletion.
- **GDPR** (if you have EU customers): right to unsubscribe.

Brote isn't legal counsel, but automatic opt-out covers the technical case of "the contact asked out and you don't keep contacting them".

## Reactivating manually

If you want to reactivate a contact (because they messaged asking to come back, or an opt-out happened by mistake), open their profile → remove the `opted_out` tag manually. From that moment they're eligible for campaigns again.

## Opt-out metrics

Each campaign shows you how many contacts opted out. If a campaign generates **\>3% opt-out**, Brote alerts you with a banner suggesting you review the message (it's a sign your cohort was too aggressive, or the message read like spam).
