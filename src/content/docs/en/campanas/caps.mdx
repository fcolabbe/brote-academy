Even if you connect WhatsApp through Meta's **official Cloud API** (the recommended path), you shouldn't send thousands of messages unrestricted — Meta detects patterns and can throttle or ban your number. To protect you, each plan has **monthly and daily caps** that Brote enforces automatically.

## Caps per plan

Campaigns are a **Pro and Business** feature. Lower plans don't have mass campaigns.

| Plan | Campaigns/month | Contacts/campaign | Messages/day (total) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Mini** ($29) | 0 — no campaigns | — | — |
| **Starter** ($49) | 0 — no campaigns | — | — |
| **Pro** ($129) | 10 | 500 | 800 |
| **Business** ($349) | unlimited | 1.500 | 2.500 |

<Aside type="caution" title="Why Mini and Starter don't have campaigns">
Mini and Starter are designed for 1:1 conversations (handling what comes in), not mass sends. Allowing campaigns on those tiers would expose inexperienced users to WA throttling or ban risk. For campaigns, Pro is the minimum.
</Aside>

## How they're counted

- **One campaign** = one scheduled send. If you launch 3 campaigns in week 1 and 4 in week 2, that's 7/month (calendar month, resets on the 1st).
- **Contacts/campaign** = unique rows in the cohort. If the same person is in 2 campaigns, they count twice.
- **Messages/day** = aggregate total across ALL active campaigns that day. If you have campaign A (drip to 50 contacts/day) + campaign B (one-shot to 100 contacts), on the day they overlap they add up to 150/day.

## Automatic throttling

When a campaign is about to exceed your daily cap:

1. Brote sends up to the day's cap.
2. The rest is **queued** for the next day.
3. It shows you "Day 1: 200/200 sent · Day 2: 0/200 (queued) · Day 3: 0/200 (queued)" in the dashboard.

If you need to go beyond the cap (e.g.: Black Friday and you have 800 contacts), you have 3 options:

1. **Upgrade your plan**: moving from Pro to Business raises the cap from 800 to 2.500/day and the cohort goes out that same day.
2. **Wait**: if there's no rush, leave it queued — 4 days later it's all sent.
3. **Shrink the cohort**: filter tighter (only "hot lead", only "returning customer").

## Why these numbers

The caps are calibrated with real data:

- **Meta tolerates ~200-300 outbound messages/day** from an unverified Business number without penalizing.
- **800/day** with good number reputation (aged account, two-way conversations) is usually sustainable.
- **2.500/day** requires a number with a strong history + a healthy inbound/outbound ratio.

If you get banned, it's not because Brote failed you — it's because WA detected something. Reconnecting the number means downtime (1-7 days) + re-verification. That's why the cap is deliberately conservative.

## WA cap vs social channel caps

The caps above apply **only to WhatsApp**. For Instagram/Messenger campaigns there are no Brote-specific caps (the platforms have their own rate limits, much lower — e.g.: IG blocks mass outreach to non-followers).

In practice, **95% of Brote campaigns go over WhatsApp** because that's where your base is.
