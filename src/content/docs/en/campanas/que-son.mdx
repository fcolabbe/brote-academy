A **Brote campaign** is a mass send of an initial message to a cohort of your contacts (e.g.: customers who bought on Black Friday 2025, leads who abandoned a cart, lapsed subscribers). The difference from a traditional blast: **the AI agent continues the conversation based on the replies**.

![Campaigns view in the dashboard](/screenshots/campaigns.png)

## The end-to-end flow

1. **You pick a cohort**: filter your contacts by tags, pipeline stage, last purchase date, vertical, etc.
2. **You write the initial message**: text + optionally media (photo/video) + optionally a pre-built payment link.
3. **You schedule the send**: now, or at a future date/time.
4. **Brote sends while respecting** your plan's caps + the contact's local waking hours + automatic opt-out.
5. **The AI agent takes over** when the contact replies — without you touching anything.
6. **Real-time tracking**: how many received, how many opened, how many replied, how many bought.

## Why the AI continuing matters

A traditional blast (Mailchimp, WA Business API template) leaves you with hundreds of replies in an inbox that NOBODY answers. Brote is different:

- If the contact says "not interested" → the AI agent closes politely + marks opt-out.
- If they say "tell me more" → the AI agent gives context + recommends a product.
- If they say "I want it" → the AI agent builds the payment link.
- If they ask something out of scope → handoff to a human with a summary.

The result: a 500-contact campaign doesn't generate 500 conversations to handle — it generates 30-50 closed sales and 450 contacts labeled by their real intent.

<Aside type="caution" title="Mandatory caps per plan">
Because Brote uses Evolution (not the Cloud API), you can't send thousands of messages in minutes without Meta banning you. Each plan has a monthly + daily cap to protect your number. See [Caps per plan](/en/campanas/caps/).
</Aside>

## Typical use cases

| Cohort | Initial message | Typical result |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 customers with no 2025 purchase | "Hi, it's been a while. We've got new pet food arrivals you'll like." | 8-15% reactivation |
| Abandoned carts, last 7 days | "I saw you left your cart. Any questions? Happy to help you close it." | 12-20% recovery |
| "Hot" leads with no purchase | "We said we'd send you info. Here it is: [link]" | 25-30% conversion |
| New product launch | "This just landed, take a look: [photo + description]" | 30-40% engagement |
| Black Friday | "The 30% off ends tomorrow. If something caught your eye, now's the time." | Weekly sales boost |

## Automatic opt-out

Every campaign includes an implicit opt-out. If a contact replies "stop", "unsubscribe", "no more messages", the AI agent:

- Closes the conversation politely.
- Marks the contact with the `opted_out` tag.
- Excludes them from **all future campaigns** automatically.
- If you ever wanted to invite them back, you'd have to revert the tag manually (you rarely want to).
