## In one sentence

**In-chat logistics** = your AI agent answers **"how much is shipping to Concepción?"** on its own, with the exact price and delivery days, and then **tracks the order** — all inside the same conversation, without opening the courier's website.

You sell products you ship to other regions. Every day your customers ask the same things: *"how much is shipping?"*, *"do you deliver to my area?"*, *"where's my order?"*. Today someone on your team opens the Chilexpress website, enters the destination and weight, copies the price and pastes it into the chat. **With Brote, the agent does it on its own in seconds**, with the real rate, 24/7.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"How much is shipping to Concepción and how long does it take?" — answered instantly by the agent, with the courier's real price, without anyone on your team lifting a finger.
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You sell **physical products** and **ship them within Chile** | You sell **services** or **digital** products (nothing to ship) |
| Your customers are in **other regions** and ask about shipping costs | You only sell and deliver **in person** at your store |
| You already have (or will have) an account with **Chilexpress** or **Starken** | You only offer **in-store pickup** |

**In plain terms:** it's for the store that ships products all over Chile and is tired of quoting shipping by hand.

<Aside type="note" title="Required plan: Starter">
In-chat logistics is included **from the Starter plan ($49/mo)**. It's not an add-on or a Business-only feature — it's available on Starter, Pro and Business. You only need to connect your courier account.
</Aside>

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **An account with a Chilean courier** — Chilexpress or Starken (you can connect both).
   - **Chilexpress:** create your 3 *subscription keys* (Coverage / Quoting / Shipments) at [developers.wschilexpress.com](https://developers.wschilexpress.com/). Free.
   - **Starken:** ask your Starken account executive for the business plan's **API credentials** (careful: they are **NOT** the same ones you use to log in to `starken.cl`).

2. **Connect the courier in Brote:** go to **Settings → Integrations → Chilexpress** (or **Starken**) and paste your credentials.

3. **Set up the shipping origin** — where your goods ship from:
   - **Chilexpress:** the origin county code (like `STGO`, `VALP`, `CONC`).
   - **Starken:** you pick your origin city from a list when connecting.

4. **An active Starter plan** (or higher).

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Starken credentials">
If you get a `401` error when connecting Starken, it's almost always because you're using the web portal credentials (`starken.cl`) instead of the API ones. They're different — ask your account executive for the API credentials.
</Aside>

2. **Camila, from Concepción, writes** on a Saturday at 10 PM: *"Hi, I want 2 kilos of coffee. How much is shipping to Concepción?"*

3. **The AI agent handles it on its own:** it recognizes "Concepción", verifies that Chilexpress has coverage, quotes 2 kg and replies right away: *"Shipping to Concepción is $4.990 and arrives in 2 to 3 business days ☕"*.

5. **On Tuesday Camila asks:** *"Where's my order?"*. Rodrigo gives Camila her tracking number and the agent replies: *"Your shipment is **out for delivery** in Concepción, it arrives today 📦"*. Without Rodrigo ever opening the Chilexpress website.

</Steps>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. The "hard" part (having a courier account) is something you already have if you ship products. Connecting in Brote means pasting your credentials once and setting where your goods ship from. **From then on the agent quotes and tracks on its own** — you don't configure anything per shipment or per customer.

Brote includes a **resolver for 80 major counties**: when the customer writes "Providencia", "Quilpué" or "Talcahuano", the agent resolves the county on its own, without asking for weird codes. And if the county falls in a rural zone outside the list, the agent **doesn't make up coverage** — it answers honestly: *"I'll confirm with the team and let you know"*.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the agent generate the shipping label?**
Not in this version. Today, in-chat logistics handles **quoting, coverage and tracking** — which is what your customers ask about every day. **Label generation** isn't included yet (it's planned for a future phase). For now you keep generating labels in your courier's portal.

**Which couriers can I connect?**
**Chilexpress** and **Starken**, Chile's two main couriers. You can connect one or both. If you connect both, Brote picks automatically (Chilexpress by default, with a configurable override if your typical customer is in areas where Starken covers better, like the south).

**How much does Brote charge me per shipment?**
Zero. Brote only quotes and tracks — you pay the shipping directly to the courier at the quoted rate. The feature is included in your plan from Starter, at no extra cost.

**Do I need to know anything technical?**
No. You paste your credentials once and that's it. The agent builds the request to the courier, resolves the county and returns the price — all under the hood.

**What if the customer asks about a rural county that's not on the list?**
The agent is honest: it doesn't invent coverage it can't confirm. It notifies you so you can coordinate and confirm with the customer.
