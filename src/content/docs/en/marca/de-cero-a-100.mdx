## In one sentence

**Brand customization** = making your AI agent and your webchat look and sound **like your business** — with your name, your tone and your colors — so the customer feels they're talking to **you**, not "a Brote bot".

Your customers never see "Brote". They see **Sofía from Tienda Aurora** replying with your brand color, your way of speaking and your logo in the chat. Brote is the invisible engine; the face is yours.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"How do I make my AI agent feel like part of my business instead of a generic chatbot?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You want the customer to perceive **your brand**, not a generic one | You don't care what the agent is called or looks like |
| You have your own **name, tone or colors** you want reflected | You're just testing and haven't defined an identity |
| You put a **webchat on your site** and want it to match your design | You only use WhatsApp and don't care about the agent's name |

**In plain terms:** it's for any business that already has an identity (or wants one) and doesn't want its automated support to feel impersonal.

<Aside type="note" title="Plan: included from the first plan">
Brand customization is **not a separate gated feature**. Naming your agent, setting its tone and personality, and adjusting the webchat's avatar and colors is **included from Mini** — it comes with building your agent. You don't need Business for this.
</Aside>

## What do you need beforehand?

<Steps>

1. **Your AI agent created** with Brote's onboarding — that's where the agent's **name** and **tone** come from automatically.

2. **A clear tone**: how you want your brand to sound (friendly, formal, humorous, direct). If you haven't defined it, no problem: the onboarding proposes one and you edit it later.

3. **Optional — your colors**: your brand's primary color and background, if you want the webchat button to match your site. Knowing the code is enough (e.g. `#C8FF1A`), or pick it in the color picker.

4. **Optional — your webchat on the site**: if you're going to customize the floating button's colors, you need the webchat embedded on your page. It's done with a snippet, no code.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
None of this requires design skills or programming. The name and tone come from the onboarding; colors are picked with a visual selector.
</Aside>

## What can you actually customize?

These are the brand pieces Brote lets you make your own today:

| Piece | Where it's configured | What it achieves |
|---|---|---|
| **Agent name** | AI agent onboarding | The customer sees "Sofía", not "assistant" |
| **Tone and register** | Agent tone block | Your brand sounds like you speak |
| **Personality and rules** | AI agent settings | The agent reflects your sales style |
| **Webchat avatar and name** | Settings → Webchat | The chat's face is your brand |
| **Webchat primary color and background** | Settings → Webchat | The button matches your site |
| **Welcome message** | Inherited from the AI agent | The greeting sounds like your business |
| **Button position** | Settings → Webchat | Fits your page's design |

<Aside type="caution" title="What it is NOT (yet)">
This is **brand customization inside Brote**, not full white-label. Documented today: agent name, tone, personality, and webchat avatar + name + colors + position. There is **no** panel on your own domain or fully hiding that the engine is Brote. If you need something like that, ask the team.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Tienda Aurora 🌱

> **Camila** owns **"Tienda Aurora"**, an online clothing store. Her brand is fresh, speaks casually to customers and uses a signature green. She wants her automated support to feel 100% Aurora, not just any bot.

<Steps>

1. **Camila builds her agent** in the onboarding. She names it **"Sofía"** and picks a warm, friendly tone. From that moment, the agent introduces itself as *"Hola, soy Sofía de Tienda Aurora 🌱"*.

2. **She adjusts the tone** in the agent's tone block: casual, short sentences, zero jargon. Sofía now speaks the way Aurora speaks on Instagram.

3. **She embeds the webchat** on her site (a snippet before the closing `body` tag). The floating chat button appears.

4. **She customizes the webchat**: sets **Aurora's green** as the primary color, uploads **Sofía's avatar**, leaves the button at the bottom right and adjusts the welcome message.

5. **A customer visits** Aurora's site. They see a green button, click, and are greeted by *"Sofía"* with the brand's face. The word "Brote" never appears.

6. **The customer asks about a dress**, Sofía replies in Aurora's tone, shares sizes and price. The customer feels they're talking to the store, not to software.

</Steps>

**Result:** the same agent that replies on WhatsApp, Instagram and the webchat does so **with a single brand voice**. The customer gets a coherent, professional experience no matter which channel they come from.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you achieve">
Your automated support stops feeling "generic". A customer who feels they're talking to **your brand** (name, tone, colors) trusts more and buys with more confidence. Same AI, but with **your face**.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. **There's nothing technical.**

- The **name and tone** are defined in the onboarding, writing in plain language.
- The **colors** are picked with a visual selector (or by pasting a code like `#C8FF1A`).
- The **avatar** is uploaded like any photo.
- The **webchat** is embedded with a snippet, without touching code.

If you can write how you want your brand to sound and pick a color, you already know how to customize Brote. It takes **less than 10 minutes**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the customer see the word "Brote"?**
Not in the conversation: they see your agent's name and avatar. The webchat can be served from `brote.ai` or your domain; the chat experience shows your brand, not Brote's.

**Can I use my own domain / full white-label?**
What's documented today is customizing the agent's name, tone, personality plus the webchat's avatar + colors + position. A full white-label on your own domain isn't documented as a self-service feature — if you need it, talk to the team.

**Do the name and tone apply to all channels?**
Yes. The agent uses the same persona and tone on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and webchat. You customize once and it applies everywhere.

**Can I change the tone later?**
Anytime. The tone lives in the agent's tone block and you edit it without redoing anything. Ideal for fine-tuning how your brand sounds.

**Do I need a designer for the colors?**
No. You pick the primary color and background with a selector. If your brand already has a color, enter that code and you're done.

**Does this cost extra?**
No. Naming the agent, setting its tone and personality, and adjusting the webchat is included from the first plan (Mini). It's not a Business feature.
