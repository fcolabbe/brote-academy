Since the official GA launch in May 2026, Brote unified its main CTA. This page documents what it is and which rules apply.

## The official CTA

> **Empieza gratis · 14 días**

Accepted variants:

- `Empieza gratis · 14 días` (default)
- `Probar gratis · 14 días`
- `Crear cuenta · 14 días gratis` (long form, OK in some contexts)

## Rules

### ✓ What we always include

- **"Gratis" (free)**: because it's a real trial, no card required.
- **"14 días" (14 days)**: to keep it concrete, not an abstract "free trial".
- **Implied plan = Starter**: the trial runs on full Starter (500 AI conversations + Shopify + MP + WA + Webchat).

### ✗ What we do NOT use

- ~~"Solicitar demo" (Request a demo)~~ — we're self-service, not enterprise sales with a form.
- ~~"Contáctanos" (Contact us)~~ — fine in footer/contact, NOT as the main CTA.
- ~~"Free forever"~~ / "Free plan forever" — we don't have a permanent free tier post-GA.
- ~~"Compra ahora" (Buy now)~~ — we don't sell one-shot, we sell a subscription.
- ~~"Beta privada" (Private beta)~~ — not anymore, we left beta in May 2026.

## Pre-GA (historical) vs Post-GA

| Stage | CTA used |
|---|---|
| Private beta (2025 - April 2026) | "Solicitar acceso" / "Beta cerrada" |
| **GA — May 2026** | **"Empieza gratis · 14 días"** |

If you find references to "private beta" in any public output (landing, email, ads, slides), it's **stale** — report or fix it.

## Allowed secondary CTAs

In specific contexts you may use:

- **"Ver demo en vivo" (See live demo)** → links to `/es/demo` (the public demo without signup).
- **"Hablar con ventas" (Talk to sales)** → on the pricing page for the Business plan (which does justify a prior conversation).
- **"Iniciar sesión" (Sign in)** → in the navbar for existing users.

None of these ever replace the main CTA **"Empieza gratis · 14 días"** in the hero, pricing cards, blog posts, or main footer.

<Aside type="tip" title="Coquimbo regional coupon">
If the audience is from Chile's Coquimbo Region, you can use the secondary CTA **"Coquimbo: USD $23/mo for 6 months"** with the coupon pre-applied. URL: `brote.ai/es/pricing?coupon=COQUIMBO`. More in [Cupón COQUIMBO](/en/planes/cupon-coquimbo/).
</Aside>

## Button styling

Main button style in the Brote brand:

- **Background**: `#C8FF1A` (lime accent)
- **Text color**: `#000` (black)
- **Weight**: `font-weight: 600`
- **Border radius**: `9999px` (pill)
- **Padding**: `0.75rem 1.5rem` (12px / 24px)
- **Hover**: lime glow effect + slight scale (1.02)

Implemented in the `action.primary` class of `brand.css` in Academy + in the `<CTA primary>` component on the landing page.
