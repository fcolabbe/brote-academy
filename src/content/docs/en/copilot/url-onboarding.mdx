If your website has good content (product descriptions, prices, About, Contact), the fastest path to configuring your AI agent is **pasting the URL** — Brote Copilot does everything else.

![Public demo at brote.ai/demo where you try the URL → agent flow](/screenshots/demo-empty.png)

## How it works

1. **You paste the full URL** of your site (e.g. `https://misitio.com`).
2. **Brote Copilot crawls** automatically:
   - Home + About + Contact + Services + Products
   - Up to ~30 relevant internal pages
   - Detects meta tags, JSON-LD Schema.org, OG tags
   - Reads visible content (not JS-rendered if it requires infinite scroll)
3. **Extracts structure**:
   - Products / services + prices
   - Hours and contact info
   - Business vertical (retail, hospitality, healthcare, etc)
   - Copy tone
4. **Synthesizes the agent**:
   - Persona (name, voice, tone)
   - Matching opening message
   - FAQs with answers
   - Typical edge cases
   - Simplified catalog
5. **Shows it to you in 60 seconds**, ready to test.

## When URL beats audio

URL onboarding is better when:

- Your site **describes your services in detail** (not just a visual landing page).
- **Prices are public** or at least ranges.
- There's a **well-written About** that helps define tone and persona.
- Your **catalog is online** (not in a private sheet).

If your site is just a landing page with a contact form, better to use the [owner's audio](/en/copilot/audio-onboarding/).

## Cases the crawl can't reach

- **JS-only sites (poorly SSR'd SPA)**: Next.js/Vue/Angular without SSR may be only partially read.
- **Sites with a paywall** or mandatory login.
- **Catalogs with many variants** that require clicking filters.
- **Products that are NOT publicly listed** (B2B).

In those cases you can **combine**: paste the URL + add the owner's audio + manually edit the generated blocks.

<Aside type="tip" title="Strongest combo: URL + audio">
For the best possible agent, **URL first** (loads catalog + structure), **audio afterwards** (adds human context + edge cases + tone). The system combines both sources and prioritizes whichever has more specific data.
</Aside>

## Re-run Copilot when you update your site

If you update your site (new prices, new products, redesign), you can **re-run Copilot** from the dashboard without losing your current config:

- It re-crawls the URL.
- Detects changes vs the previous version.
- Shows you a **diff** of what it would propose changing.
- You accept/reject change by change (same as [Copilot Audit](/en/copilot/audit/)).

This lets you keep the agent up to date without reconfiguring from scratch every time.
