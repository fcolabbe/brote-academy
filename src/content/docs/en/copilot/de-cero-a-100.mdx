## In one sentence

**Brote Copilot** is the assistant that **builds your AI agent for you**: give it your **website URL** or send it an **audio about your business**, and in **15 minutes** it leaves the agent configured —information, tone and tools— without you writing a single prompt.

And it doesn't end there: after your agent talks with real customers, Copilot **audits** those conversations and suggests concrete improvements that you accept or reject.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question it answers">
"I want an AI agent to serve my customers, but I know nothing technical and have no time to configure it by hand. How do I build it fast?"
</Aside>

## Who is it for?

| It's for you if… | You don't need it (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You're just starting with Brote and **don't want to configure by hand** | You already have your agent fine-tuned and don't want to touch it |
| **You don't know how to write prompts** and don't want to learn | You're technical and prefer editing every block by hand |
| You have a **website** (or Instagram/landing page) with your business info | You have nothing online **and** no desire to record an audio |
| You'd rather **talk for 2 minutes** than fill out forms | — |

**In plain terms:** it's for **anyone starting out** who wants their agent running now, without fighting with configuration.

<Aside type="note" title="Plan required: all">
Brote Copilot is **Brote's onboarding** — it's available on **all plans** (Mini, Starter, Pro, Business). It's the front door: this is how you build your agent on day one, regardless of your plan.
</Aside>

## What do you need before starting?

Not much. **One** of these two things is enough:

<Steps>

1. **Your site's URL** — your website, your online store or even your Instagram profile. Ideal if it already has products, prices, an "About" page or contact info.

2. **Or the willingness to record a 2-min audio** — if your site is very basic or you don't have one, say out loud what you sell, to whom and how you charge. Like explaining it to a new employee.

3. **Nothing technical.** No need to know about prompts, or AI, or copying code. Copilot does all the heavy lifting.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Which one do I choose?">
If your site **describes** your services and prices well → **URL** (faster). If your website is just a visual landing page or a contact form → **audio**. Want the best of both? Paste the URL first and add an audio later: Copilot combines both sources.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Camila 🌱

> **Camila** owns **"Mundo Michi"**, a premium cat food and accessories store in Coquimbo. She sells through Instagram and WhatsApp. She knows nothing about programming and until today answered every message by hand.

<Steps>

1. **Camila logs into Brote and pastes her store's URL** (`https://mundomichi.cl`). She doesn't configure anything else.

2. **Brote Copilot reads her site on its own:** it goes through the home page, the products, the "About" page and the contact page. It extracts products, prices, hours and detects her vertical is **pet retail**.

3. **In 60 seconds, Copilot shows her the agent already built:** a name and a warm tone matching her brand, a welcome message, the typical FAQs (shipping, payment methods, returns) and what to do when it doesn't know something (hand off to Camila).

4. **Camila tests it** in the playground, tweaks a couple of details and connects it to her WhatsApp. **From zero to a live agent: under 15 minutes.**

5. **A week later**, the agent has already talked with dozens of customers. Camila taps **"Audit now"** and Copilot reviews those real conversations.

6. **Copilot suggests concrete improvements:** *"3 customers asked if you ship to La Serena and the agent answered differently each time → add this FAQ"* and *"2 customers wanted to buy and the agent didn't send them the payment link → enable this tool"*. Camila reads each suggestion, accepts the ones that help and rejects one. **In 2 minutes her agent was better than on day 1.**

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what happened">
Camila **never wrote a prompt** or touched a technical setting. She pasted a URL, tested, connected, and let Copilot keep fine-tuning the agent with real conversations. That's "from zero to 100" without being technical.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy: **you don't configure anything by hand.**

- **You don't write prompts.** Copilot generates them from your URL or your audio.
- **You don't copy code** or touch anything technical.
- **You don't build the agent's persona** — Copilot deduces name, tone and welcome message from your own business.
- **You don't guess what to improve** — the audit tells you exactly where the agent failed and what to change, with the reason.

Your only job is **saying "yes" or "no"** to what Copilot proposes. It never changes your agent without your OK.

<Aside type="note" title="What if I make a mistake or my business changes?">
You can **re-run Copilot** whenever you want (you raised prices, added new products, redesigned the site). It shows you a diff of what it would propose changing and you accept change by change, without reconfiguring from scratch.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know tech or AI?**
No. That's exactly the point of Copilot: you talk about your business and it translates that into a working agent. Zero prompts, zero code.

**How long does it really take?**
The agent is built in **15 minutes**. Processing the audio or crawling your site takes a few minutes; the rest is you testing it and connecting it.

**Can I try it before creating an account?**
Yes. There's a public demo: paste any URL and see the generated agent in 60 seconds, without signing up.

**What if my site is very basic or I don't have one?**
Record a 2-5 minute audio saying what you sell, to whom and how you charge. Copilot transcribes it and builds the agent all the same.

**Does the agent stay like it was on day 1?**
No. The **audit** reads real conversations and proposes improvements. In 30 days your agent answers quite a bit better than at the start.

**Does Copilot change my agent without telling me?**
Never. Every suggestion goes through your review: you accept, edit or reject each one before applying it.

**Which plan is it in?**
All of them. It's Brote's onboarding, so you have it from day one regardless of your plan.
