When your website is basic (or you don't have one), the fastest path to configuring your AI agent is recording a **2-5 minute audio** explaining your business. Brote Copilot transcribes + classifies + builds the agent.

## The perfect audio

Brote Copilot gets the most out of your audio if you cover these 5 blocks:

### 1. What you sell / what service you offer (~30s)

> "I'm [your name], owner of [business name]. I sell [product/service] in [city/region]. My customers are [customer type]."

Example: "Hi, I'm María, owner of Tienda Brote in Coquimbo. I sell premium pet food and cat accessories. My customers are cat owners looking for quality without paying pharmacy prices."

### 2. Your typical products/services (~60s)

> "My best-selling products are [3-5 products with prices]. I also offer [additional services]."

Mention:
- 3-5 star products/services with approximate prices
- Premium vs budget products
- Subscription products if you have them
- Categories you do NOT handle (so the agent knows when to hand off)

### 3. Business rules (~60s)

> "For payment I accept [payment methods]. My hours are [hours]. I ship to [areas] and it takes [timeframes]. My return policy is [policy]."

Also mention:
- Whether you have a minimum order
- Whether you offer free delivery above a certain amount
- Whether you have warranties or exchanges

### 4. Tone you want the agent to use (~30s)

> "I want the agent to talk like [style]. It should be [adjectives: warm/professional/fun/formal]. It should use [informal/formal address]. It should [not use voseo / use Chilean slang / use Spanglish]."

If your brand already has a defined tone, mention it. If not, just speak naturally — the copilot deduces it from your own voice.

### 5. Special cases / handoff (~60s)

> "When the customer asks about [complex cases], hand off to a human. When someone asks about [confidential info], reply [answer]. When a customer is upset, [action]."

Useful so the agent does NOT mess up in sensitive cases.

## Recording tips

- **Quiet environment**: the model transcribes better without background noise.
- **Speak naturally**: it's not a script, it's like briefing a new employee.
- **Repeat key data**: prices, hours, contact info — saying them twice fixes them better.
- **Don't read**: if you read, you sound formal and the agent imitates you. Converse.
- **Pause between blocks**: it helps the model separate sections.

<Aside type="tip" title="How long should it be?">
**2-5 minutes is the sweet spot.** Under 2 min usually lacks context. Over 5 min gets repetitive and dilutes what matters. If you naturally end up at 7-8 min, that's OK.
</Aside>

## What Brote Copilot does with your audio

1. **Transcribes** (Whisper) → text.
2. **Classifies** sections (products, rules, tone, special cases).
3. **Detects your vertical** automatically.
4. **Generates 6 agent_blocks**: persona, instruction, edge_cases, FAQs, simplified catalog, tone.
5. **Synthesizes an opening message** aligned with your business.
6. Shows you the agent ready to test in the [Playground](/en/empezar/primer-agente/).

All in **2-5 minutes of processing**.
