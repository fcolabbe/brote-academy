**Brote Copilot** is Brote's core innovation: it configures your conversational AI agent **in 15 minutes** without you writing prompts or hiring a consultant.

## How it works

There are two paths to configuring your agent:

### 1. Paste your site's URL

Brote Copilot:
- Reads your home page + internal pages (Contact, About, Services)
- Extracts products, prices, services, contact info, hours
- Detects your vertical (retail, hospitality, healthcare, etc.)
- Synthesizes the agent's persona (name, tone, opening message)
- Configures frequent FAQs with answers
- Defines edge cases (what to do when it doesn't know, when to hand off to a human)

[How URL onboarding works →](/en/copilot/url-onboarding/)

### 2. Record a 2-5 minute audio

If your website is basic or you don't have one:
- You describe what you sell, who your customers are, typical prices, business rules
- Brote transcribes + classifies + builds the agent

[How to record the audio →](/en/copilot/audio-onboarding/)

## After the initial setup

- **[Automatic audit](/en/copilot/audit/)**: Brote Copilot scans real conversations and detects gaps (frequent questions without a good answer, unnecessary escalations, missed sales opportunities).
- **Quick-tunes with review**: the copilot proposes corrections and you accept/reject them before they're applied. It never changes your agent without your OK.
- **Re-run Copilot**: if you updated your site or your business, a single click regenerates.

<Aside type="tip" title="Continuous improvement">
The AI agent does NOT stay static. Brote Copilot Audit keeps refining it based on real conversations with your customers. In 30 days your agent answers better than on day 1.
