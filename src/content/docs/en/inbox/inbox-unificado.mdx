Brote's unified Inbox brings every conversation from every channel into a single view. Your team doesn't have to open WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger separately — everything lives here.

![Unified Inbox with conversations from multiple channels](/screenshots/inbox.png)

## What you see in the Inbox

Each row in the list is a conversation with:

- **Avatar and initials** of the contact (or "Web visitor" if they're still anonymous)
- **Name** of the contact (if we have it)
- **Last message** received or sent, with a preview
- **Channel**: icon badge (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Webchat). Email and Telegram are coming soon.
- **AI vs Human status**: a pill showing whether the agent is handling it or it's in handoff
- **Tags**: labels you created in [Tags and custom fields](/en/inbox/tags-fields/)

## Available filters

Above the list:

- **All channels** / WhatsApp / Instagram / Messenger / Webchat
- **All statuses** / Unread / With AI / With human / Closed
- **All agents** (if your team has more than one) / a specific agent
- **Free-text search** by name, phone, email, or message content

<Aside type="tip" title="Realtime, no refresh needed">
The Inbox uses **Supabase Realtime** over WebSocket. When a new message arrives, it appears at the top of the list without you having to refresh. If you're inside a conversation, new messages show up at the end of the thread with an optional sound ping.
</Aside>

## How your team operates it

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Superadmin** (owner) | Everything: inbox + settings + billing + admins |
| **Admin** | Inbox + settings + invite members (no billing) |
| **Operator (human agent)** | Only assigned conversations + change status + reply |

Assignment: if a conversation enters **handoff** (the AI agent escalated it), the system assigns it to the operator with the lightest load, or to a fixed one if you configured round-robin/preferred.

## Taking over from the AI

Any member with permissions can **pause the AI** in a conversation and reply manually. There are 3 ways:

1. Click the "AI on/off" toggle above the thread.
2. Type a manual message — it automatically sets the AI to `paused`.
3. Move the conversation to "human" status on the kanban.

When the conversation is closed (won / lost / archived), the AI goes back to `active` for future interactions with the same contact.

## Quick actions on each conversation

- Mark as **read / unread**
- **Assign** to another team member
- **Move in the pipeline** (changes the stage of the linked ticket)
- **Add a tag** or **create an internal note**
- **Send a payment link** (if you have Mercado Pago connected)
- **Close** as won / lost / archived
