## In one sentence

**Unified Inbox + kanban pipeline** = **one single inbox** with the conversations from WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Webchat, organized as **cards on a sales board**, where the AI agent replies on its own and **passes the case to your team** right when it's needed.

Instead of your team hopping between WhatsApp on a phone, Instagram DMs and the Messenger inbox — losing messages along the way — everything comes into **one place**. Each conversation is a ticket that moves through stages (New lead → In contact → Human handling → Won), and when the customer needs a person, the bot hands off with a summary ready.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"People message me on WhatsApp, Instagram and the web, and my team loses messages. How do I see **everything in one place** and make sure **nobody goes unanswered**?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | It's NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| Customers write to you on **more than one channel** (WhatsApp + Instagram + Messenger + web) | Everything is in-person, no messages |
| You have (or will have) **a team** answering, and they sometimes clash or lose messages | It's just you with a single WhatsApp and very few messages |
| You want a **sales board** showing what stage each customer is in | You're not interested in organizing the sales process |
| You need the bot to handle things but **hand off to a human** in tough cases | You want 100% automated replies without ever stepping in |

**In plain terms:** it's for the business that **gets messages from all over and wants to stop losing sales** to chaos.

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Javiera 🌱

> **Javiera** owns **"Deco Nube"**, a home decor store that sells through **WhatsApp and Instagram**. Three people handle messages. The problem: one person sees the Instagram messages, another sees WhatsApp, and **every week at least one sale falls through** because someone "thought the other had already replied".

### Before (the chaos)

- WhatsApp open on one salesperson's phone.
- Instagram DMs on another's phone.
- A customer who asked about a couch on Monday **went unanswered for 2 days** → bought elsewhere.
- Nobody knew what stage each conversation was in.

### After (from zero to 100 with Brote)

<Steps>

1. **Javiera connects WhatsApp and Instagram** to Brote. Now both channels flow into **a single inbox** — the [unified Inbox](/en/inbox/inbox-unificado/). Her team stops opening separate apps.

2. **A message comes in via Instagram:** *"Hi, do you have the gray couch in stock?"*. It appears **at the top of the list instantly** (realtime, no reloading), with the Instagram badge and the "AI handling" status.

3. **The AI agent replies on its own:** shows the couch, the price ($180.000), available colors, and automatically creates a **ticket in "New lead"** on the kanban board. When the customer shares more details, the ticket moves to **"In contact"** on its own.

4. **Another customer gets tricky on WhatsApp:** wants an invoice, a discount for 4 units and shipping to another region. The agent detects it's a **complex case** and does a **handoff**: moves the ticket to **"Human handling"** (the yellow column) and **notifies the team** with a push.

5. **Camila (operator) opens the ticket** and sees the **auto-generated summary**: who the customer is, what they asked for, what was offered and the detected intent. **She doesn't have to read 30 messages** — she replies in 1 minute and closes the sale.

6. **At the end, she drags the ticket to "Won".** Who moved it and when is recorded. The AI goes back to `active` for the next time that customer writes.

</Steps>

**A month later, Javiera looks at her board and sees the numbers:**

| Metric | Before | With Brote |
|---|---|---|
| Lost messages / week | ~3-4 | **0** |
| Time to first reply | up to 2 days | **under 5 minutes** (bot replies on its own) |
| Conversations reaching "Won" | they didn't know | **visible on the board** |
| Apps the team opens | 3 (WA, IG, phone) | **1 (Brote)** |

**Javiera's takeaway:** *"We recover at least 3 sales a month that used to slip away on their own. At $180.000 average, that's over $500.000 a month we were giving away to chaos."* All that, without hiring anyone else.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
You stop losing sales to forgotten messages. The bot handles the volume, your team steps in only where it adds value, and **you see on a board where every sale stands**. It's selling more with the same team.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Which plan includes it?**
**All of them**, starting with Mini. The unified Inbox, the kanban pipeline, handoff and internal notes are Brote core. What varies by plan are the official channels (Instagram/Messenger from Starter) and how many team members you can have.

**Do messages appear on their own or do I have to refresh?**
They appear on their own, in real time. The Inbox uses a live connection: when a new message comes in, it rises to the top of the list without you touching anything (with an optional sound ping).

**Does the bot answer everything or do I have to be around?**
The AI agent replies on its own by default. You (or any member with permission) can **pause the AI** in any conversation and take over: just write a manual message or hit the "AI on/off" toggle. When you close the conversation, the AI switches back on.

**How do I know when a customer needs a human?**
The AI agent does an automatic **handoff** when the customer asks for it, when the question is out of its scope, when it detects frustration or when it smells a hot lead. At that moment it moves the ticket to the human column and alerts your team via push (and optionally email or Slack).

**Can I move conversations by hand on the board?**
Yes. Any operator can **drag a ticket** from one column to another. Who moved it and when is saved, for your sales-cycle reports.

**Can I have different sales flows at the same time?**
Yes. You can run **several pipelines** in parallel (e.g. one for sales, another for support, another for subscriptions). Each conversation lands in the pipeline it belongs to based on what the agent detects, and you can move it if needed.

**What about anonymous web customers?**
They appear as "Visitante web" until they share their name or phone. Then Brote recognizes them and unifies their history with the rest of their channels.
