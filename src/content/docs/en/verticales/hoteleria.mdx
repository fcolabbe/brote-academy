For hospitality and tourism, Brote acts as the **virtual front desk** that covers all 4 stages of the guest journey — without overloading your real team.

## The typical flow

1. **Pre-booking**: guest asks about availability, prices, photos, policies → agent answers and captures details.
2. **Booking**: guest confirms → agent sends an MP payment link / preapproval / deposit.
3. **Pre-stay**: day before → agent sends check-in info, address, WiFi code, host details.
4. **Post-stay**: day after → agent asks for a review, offers a discount for the next booking.

## Recommended setup

### Custom fields

```
fecha_check_in (date)
fecha_check_out (date)
huespedes_adultos (number)
huespedes_ninos (number)
tipo_habitacion (select: standard / suite / departamento)
pais_origen (text)
es_repetido (boolean)
ocasion (select: pareja / familia / negocios / amigos)
```

### Useful tags

`reserva_confirmada`, `pago_pendiente`, `check_in_hoy`, `repetido`, `oferta_diciembre`, `lead_temporada_alta`

### Key integrations

- **[Google Calendar](/en/integraciones/calendar/)**: the agent checks your real availability and blocks booked dates.
- **[Mercado Pago](/en/pagos/mercado-pago/)**: deposit + balance payments. Preapproval for rates with a cancellation policy.
- **[Webhooks/Zapier](/en/integraciones/zapier/)**: when a booking is confirmed → your PMS (Cloudbeds, Mews, Octorate) gets the data.

## Conversation example

```
Cliente: Hola, tienen para 2 personas del 15 al 18 de junio?

Agente: Hola! Sí, tenemos disponibilidad esos días. Te puedo ofrecer:
- Habitación standard: $85.000 por noche
- Suite con vista al mar: $135.000 por noche
Las 2 incluyen desayuno y WiFi.
Qué te tinca más?

Cliente: La standard

Agente: Perfecto. Para 3 noches (15, 16, 17) son $255.000 totales.
Te mando el link de pago con depósito del 30% ($76.500). El saldo
lo pagas al hacer check-in.
[link MP]

Cliente: [paga]

Agente: ✅ Recibe tu depósito. Reserva confirmada para Camila Vega
del 15 al 18 de junio, habitación standard.
Te escribo el día 14 con info de check-in. Cualquier cambio, avisame!
```

<Aside type="tip" title="Dynamic rates">
If your business uses dynamic pricing (high/low season, weekends), configure the agent with a **"tarifas" agent_block** detailing the rules. The agent quotes correctly without you updating anything by hand.
</Aside>

## Metrics to watch

- **Average response time**: in hospitality you compete with OTAs (Booking, Airbnb). If your agent takes less than 1 min, you win direct bookings (no commission).
- **Inquiry-to-booking conversion rate**: boutique hotel benchmark = 18-25%. With Brote well configured you reach 30-35%.
- **Repeat rate** (returning guests): post-stay follow-up with a discount moves this to 15-20%.
