Brote doesn't charge its customers directly — it uses **[Polar](https://polar.sh)** as Merchant of Record (MoR). Polar is legally responsible for charging you, issuing the invoice with your country's VAT and moving the money.

## Why Polar and not Stripe

For a traditional LATAM SMB, going with Stripe directly means:
- A bank account in USD or in a supported jurisdiction.
- Manual tax compliance (VAT, withholdings).
- Currency conversion where the customer pays in USD even if they live in CLP.

**Polar solves this by being the merchant**:
- **Charges in your local currency**: CLP in Chile, MXN in Mexico, ARS in Argentina, COP in Colombia, BRL in Brazil, USD in the US, EUR in Spain. No surprise currency conversion.
- **Issues the invoice with the correct VAT**: 19% Chile, 16% Mexico, 21% Argentina, 19% Colombia, etc. You just receive a clean invoice.
- **Handles compliance**: if you sell in 7 countries, you don't need tax registration in 7 — Polar absorbs it as MoR.

## How it works in practice

1. You pick a plan (Mini / Starter / Pro / Business) at `brote.ai/es/pricing`.
2. Click "Empieza gratis · 14 días" → it takes you to the Polar checkout.
3. You enter your **country** + **minimal tax details**.
4. Polar calculates the final price with your country's VAT + currency.
5. You pay with card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) or supported local methods (MercadoPago in some countries).
6. Polar issues the invoice → the PDF arrives in your inbox.
7. Brote receives the `subscription.created` webhook → your account is activated with the chosen plan.

## The trial fits here too

During the 14-day [trial](/en/planes/trial/) we charge you NOTHING. Polar accepts a card as a payment method but **doesn't charge until day 14** (if you chose to continue).

If you did NOT choose to continue → on day 14 your account enters [read-only mode](/en/planes/trial/) and Polar doesn't charge you.

## Plan changes

If you change plans (e.g. Starter → Pro), Polar:
- Prorates the cost of the change (charges you only the difference from the day of the change).
- Issues a **supplementary invoice** for the difference.
- Your next cycle starts on the new plan.

Same logic when downgrading (Pro → Starter): Polar refunds prorated credit or applies it to the next cycle.

## Coupons and discounts

Polar supports **discount codes**. The [Cupón COQUIMBO](/en/planes/cupon-coquimbo/) is implemented as a Polar Discount applicable to the Starter plan, with `duration: repeating` for 6 months. Other future coupons (summer promo, B2B partners) are managed from Brote's Polar dashboard.

<Aside type="tip" title="Refunds and cancellations">
**Cancel anytime**, no call or form: a "Cancel subscription" button in your dashboard. Polar processes the cancellation same-day. Your access stays active until the end of the current paid cycle.
</Aside>

## Difference from Mercado Pago

**Polar** = you pay your Brote subscription (B2B SaaS).
**[Mercado Pago](/en/pagos/mercado-pago/)** = you charge your end customers (your business).

They're two different things. Polar is the SaaS charging you. MP is the gateway YOU use to charge your customers.
