If your SMB is in Chile's **Coquimbo Region** — La Serena, Coquimbo, Ovalle, Vicuña, Illapel — the **COQUIMBO** coupon gets you the Starter plan at a reduced price for 6 months.

## What you get

- **Full Starter plan** (500 AI conversations, WhatsApp + Webchat, Shopify, Mercado Pago, campaigns, pipeline)
- **USD $26/mo discount for 6 months** (leaving it at USD $23/mo ≈ $21.600 CLP)
- **No card** during the prior 14-day trial
- From month 7 on, it returns to the regular Starter price (USD $49/mo)

## How to apply it

### Option 1 — URL with the coupon auto-applied (easiest)

Open this link and you'll see the discount already applied when choosing Starter:

[brote.ai/es/pricing?coupon=COQUIMBO](https://brote.ai/es/pricing?coupon=COQUIMBO)

![Cupón COQUIMBO applied on pricing](/screenshots/pricing-coquimbo-applied.png)

### Option 2 — Manual

1. Go to [brote.ai/es/signup](https://brote.ai/es/signup) and create your account.
2. Activate the 14-day trial (no card).
3. When choosing the Starter plan at checkout, enter the code `COQUIMBO` in the coupon field.
4. Verify you see the discount applied before confirming.

<Aside type="caution" title="Limitations">
- Only applies to the **Starter** plan (not Mini, Pro or Business).
- Only during the customer's **first 6 months**. After that, regular price of $49/mo.
- No global redemption limit, but **only once per organization**.
</Aside>

## Why this discount

Brote was born in Coquimbo. As a commitment to the regional SMB ecosystem, we offer this discount so companies in the area can access conversational AI without a price barrier.

It's part of our sustainability commitment: we want **30+ SMBs in Coquimbo** using Brote during 2026.

## Does my business qualify?

The coupon is public — technically anyone can use it. But the spirit is for **companies in Chile's Coquimbo Region**. If your SMB is outside the region and you want to use it anyway, go ahead — we just ask that you tell us where you're writing from at [hola@brote.ai](mailto:hola@brote.ai).
