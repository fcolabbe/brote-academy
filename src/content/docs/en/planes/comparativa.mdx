## If you run out of quota (soft overage)

The service is **never cut off** for exceeding your plan's conversations. When you hit the cap:
- The agent **keeps replying** (nothing pauses).
- You see the **overage** in Settings → Billing.
- You can **add a conversation pack** for that month without changing plans: **+500 for $15** or **+2,000 for $40**. It adds to your quota and doesn't expire until the end of the month.

That way you upgrade plans for *new capability* (features), not because you ran out of quota.

## WhatsApp credits (Cloud API)

If you send campaigns via **official WhatsApp (Cloud API)**, Meta charges a fee per marketing/utility message. Brote covers it with **prepaid credits** (packs of $25/$50/$100 of coverage) that are only deducted for Cloud API — with the **standard connection (Evolution) nothing is deducted**. Also, messages sent **within the 24-hour window** (or 72h after a Click-to-WhatsApp ad) are **free** and don't consume credit: *Brote minimizes your WhatsApp bill automatically.*
