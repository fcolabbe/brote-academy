## What's included

When you create your account you start with **14 free days of the full Starter plan**:

- 500 AI conversations with your agent
- WhatsApp + Webchat active
- Full Shopify + Mercado Pago integration
- Kanban pipeline + tags + notes
- 2 team members
- WhatsApp campaigns (2/mo · 100 contacts)
- 100 MB knowledge base

![Pricing with plans](/screenshots/pricing-plans.png)

## No credit card

We don't ask for a card to start. Not on day 14 either, if you don't want to continue.

## Notices during the trial

- **Day 7**: banner in the dashboard letting you know you have 7 days left.
- **Day 12**: email + critical banner "2 days left, add a card to keep access".
- **Day 14**: your trial expires.

## What happens on day 14

### If you added a card + chose a plan
Your account continues as normal with the plan you chose (it can be the same Starter, or downgrade to Mini, or upgrade to Pro/Business).

### If you did NOT add a card
Your account enters **read-only mode** for 30 more days:

- **You can**: view your conversations, contacts, pipeline, agent settings, all your history.
- **You canNOT**: receive new messages (the AI agent is paused). Messages coming into your WhatsApp/Webchat are NOT processed by Brote.

### After 30 days in read-only mode
Your account is **archived** automatically. Your data is preserved for 180 more days in case you decide to come back. After that, it's deleted per the privacy policy.

<Aside type="tip" title="Reactivate your account">
If your account is in read-only mode or archived, you can reactivate it anytime by adding a card + choosing a plan. All your data becomes available again.
</Aside>

## Can I cancel earlier?
Yes, anytime. No lock-in, no forms, no calls. Click _"Cancel subscription"_ from the dashboard.

## How am I billed after the trial?

We work with **[Polar as Merchant of Record](/en/planes/polar/)**: they charge in your local currency (CLP, MXN, ARS, COP, USD…), handle per-country VAT automatically and issue the invoice. Brote never touches your money.
