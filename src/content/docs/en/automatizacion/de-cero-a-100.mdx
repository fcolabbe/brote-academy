## In one sentence

**Automation** = Brote **does things on its own** when something happens, without you watching your phone.

A customer abandons their cart → Brote messages them 2 hours later with a discount. Someone writes to you for the first time → they get a welcome greeting instantly. An appointment is coming up → the reminder goes out. A payment is approved → the ticket closes and gets tagged on its own. **You define the "when X happens, do Y" once, and it runs 24/7** — while you sleep, while you help another customer, while you're on vacation.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"How many sales am I losing because nobody replied in time, nobody followed up, nobody remembered the appointment?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| Sales slip away because **you can't keep up with follow-ups** | You handle everything yourself and always reply instantly |
| You have **repeatable events** (carts, appointments, payments, first contacts) | Every conversation is unique and there's no pattern |
| You want the business to **sell and retain on autopilot** | You're just now connecting your first channel |

**Plainly put:** it's for the business that already has momentum and wants Brote to **work on its own** on the things that get forgotten or done late: the follow-up, the welcome, the reminder, the close.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: Pro">
Automations (along with campaigns and Brote Copilot) are part of the **Pro** plan (USD $129/mo). On Mini and Starter you'll see the feature with a lock. Upgrading to Pro unlocks them. Scheduling reminders are the exception: they work from Starter because they come bundled with the appointments module.
</Aside>

## What do you need before building your first automation?

<Steps>

1. **A connected channel** — at least WhatsApp ([how to connect it](/en/canales/whatsapp/)). It's where Brote will write when the automation fires.

2. **Your AI agent configured** — the one that replies and continues the conversation when the customer answers. If you haven't set it up yet, [start here](/en/empezar/agente-de-cero-a-100/) or use [Brote Copilot](/en/copilot/que-es/) to get it ready in minutes.

3. **Contacts with data** — a phone number and, for abandoned carts, knowing which product they left behind. All of this fills in on its own via the [agent tools](/en/automatizacion/tools-del-agente/) (`update_contact`, `assign_tag`) as it chats.

4. An active **Pro plan** (or the 14-day trial to test it).

5. **Optional but recommended:** a [connected payment gateway](/en/pagos/de-cero-a-100/) (Mercado Pago, Flow or Khipu) so the automation can send the payment link and close the sale right there.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
No code knowledge or external tools needed. Simple automations are built inside Brote. If you want highly sophisticated flows with your own systems, you can later add [Make, n8n or Zapier](/en/integraciones/make-n8n/) — but you don't need them to start.
</Aside>

## How it works: trigger → action

Every automation is the same simple idea: **a trigger** (something that happens) connected to **an action** (something Brote does).

**Most-used triggers:**

| When this happens… | It's called |
|---|---|
| A customer writes to you for the first time | `message_received` (first contact) |
| A customer leaves a cart unpaid | abandoned cart (`carrito-abandonado` tag) |
| A payment is approved | `payment_received` |
| The contact advances a stage | `lifecycle_changed` |
| A ticket closes as won | `ticket_won` |
| A scheduled appointment is coming up | scheduled reminder |

**Actions Brote can take on its own** (via the [agent tools](/en/automatizacion/tools-del-agente/)):

- Send a message (with or without a photo, PDF or audio).
- Send a ready-made payment link (`build_payment_link`).
- Tag the contact (`assign_tag`) or move them to another stage (`change_stage`).
- Hand the ticket to a team member if the topic calls for it (`handoff_to_human`).

And Brote's extra magic: **when the customer replies, the AI agent takes over** and continues the conversation on its own. It doesn't leave you 200 unanswered replies in your inbox.

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Trotamundos 🌱

> **Diego** runs **"Trotamundos"**, an online outdoor sneaker store. He sells over WhatsApp and Instagram. His pain: lots of people build a cart, ask the price… and vanish. Nobody follows up and those sales go cold.

Diego builds **a single automation** for abandoned carts:

<Steps>

1. **The trigger:** when a customer builds a cart (the agent already tags them `carrito-abandonado`) and **2 hours pass without payment**.

2. **The action:** Brote messages them on WhatsApp, on its own:
   *"Hi Valentina 👟 I saw you left the Trekking Pro in your cart. I'll give you 10% off if we close it today: they're down to $45.000. Want me to send the payment link?"*

3. **Valentina replies** "sure". The **AI agent takes over**: confirms the size, applies the discount and sends her the **Mercado Pago payment link** (`build_payment_link`).

4. **Valentina pays.** The approved payment fires another small automation: the ticket moves to **closed-won** and gets tagged `cliente-recurrente`. Diego touched nothing.

</Steps>

**The previous month, without automation:**

| | Without automation |
|---|---|
| Abandoned carts | 120 |
| Recovered (manual follow-up, too late) | 6 |
| Recovered sales | $270.000 |

**The first month with the abandoned-cart automation:**

| | With automation |
|---|---|
| Abandoned carts | 120 |
| Recovered (Brote writes at the 2h mark, on its own) | 22 |
| Recovered sales | **$990.000** 🎉 |

**The difference:** $720.000 more per month, from sales that used to go cold. All with **one automation Diego built once** that runs on its own. The Pro plan that enables it costs less than **one** of those recovered sneakers.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
You stop losing sales to "I didn't get to reply". The follow-up you **never** do on time (because you're selling, shipping or sleeping) happens at the exact right second, done by Brote. Sell more **without working more**.
</Aside>

## Other typical automations (same pattern)

| You want… | Trigger | Brote's action |
|---|---|---|
| Every new customer to get a welcome | Customer's first message | Greeting + business intro |
| To cut no-shows on your appointments | 24h / 2h before the appointment | [Automatic reminder](/en/agendamiento/recordatorios/) over WhatsApp |
| To reactivate dormant customers | No purchase in 90 days | "It's been a while — check this out" message |
| To thank customers after purchase | Payment approved | "Thanks for your purchase! I'm here if you need anything" |
| To prioritize hot leads | Contact reaches "proposal" stage | `vip` tag + team notification |

<Aside type="note">
Want to send a mass message to a whole list (not an event-based trigger)? Those are [campaigns](/en/campanas/que-son/) — a Black Friday reactivation, a launch announcement, recovering last week's carts in bulk. Automation and campaigns complement each other: one reacts to events, the other goes out to your base.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. Simple automations are built inside Brote by picking a trigger and an action. Zero code.

**Does automation send spam?**
No. Brote respects your plan's [messaging caps](/en/campanas/caps/), the contact's local waking hours and automatic opt-out. If someone says "no more messages", they're excluded automatically.

**What happens when the customer replies to an automated message?**
The **AI agent takes over** and continues the conversation on its own: it answers questions, builds the cart, sends the payment link or hands off to a human if the topic calls for it. It doesn't leave your inbox full of unanswered replies.

**Which plan includes it?**
**Pro** (USD $129/mo), along with campaigns and Copilot. Appointment reminders work from Starter because they come with the scheduling module.

**Can I connect external systems (my ERP, my CRM)?**
Yes, with more advanced flows via [Make, n8n or Zapier](/en/integraciones/make-n8n/) (Pro and up) or [Webhooks + API](/en/integraciones/webhooks-api/) (Business). But for day-to-day automations you don't need any of that.

**What if I'm still selling very little?**
Automation pays off when you already have repeatable activity (carts, appointments, first contacts). If you're just starting, focus first on [connecting your WhatsApp](/en/canales/whatsapp/) and [setting up your agent](/en/empezar/agente-de-cero-a-100/).
