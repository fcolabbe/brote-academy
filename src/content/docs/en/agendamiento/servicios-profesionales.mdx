Built for **professional services sold by time** (consultations, advisory, sessions, therapy, veterinary care, law firms). It adds three things on top of the [base scheduling](/en/agendamiento/configurar/): an **address** per service, a **per-professional calendar**, and **charge to confirm** the appointment.

Everything is configured in **Settings → Scheduling**. Available from the **Starter** plan.

## Service address

Each service can have an **address** (e.g. "Av. Providencia 1234, of. 4"). When the agent proposes or confirms an appointment, it shares it with the customer automatically. If the service is online, leave it blank.

## Per-professional calendar

If you have **several professionals** (or boxes/rooms) working in parallel, each one can have **their own hours** and handle **only certain services**.

<Steps>

1. **Add the professionals** in the **Resources (team)** section: e.g. "Dr. Rojas", "Dr. Muñoz".

2. **Assign who handles each service.** On each service you'll see **"Handled by:"** chips — check the professionals who provide that service. If you check none, **everyone** handles it.

3. **Give each one their hours.** In **Weekly availability**, use the **"Editing the schedule of:"** selector:
   - **Org shared schedule** → the common schedule for everyone.
   - **A professional** → their own schedule (e.g. Dr. Rojas Mon-Wed-Fri 09–18, Dr. Muñoz Tue-Thu 10–19). If a professional has no schedule of their own, they **inherit the org's shared one**.

</Steps>

With this, the agent handles two ways of booking in the chat:

- **"I want Dr. Rojas"** → the agent shows **that professional's calendar**.
- **"Who's free on Tuesday?"** → the agent lists **the professionals available** that day.

Every slot it offers indicates **which professional** handles it, and the booking **blocks only that professional's calendar** (two different customers can take the same time with different professionals).

<Aside type="note">
If you don't add professionals, everything works as a single calendar (the classic behavior). Professionals are optional.
</Aside>

## Charge to confirm the appointment

To reduce **no-shows**, you can require **upfront payment**: the appointment isn't confirmed until the customer pays.

### How to enable it

On the service, check **💳 Charge to confirm** and set the **hold minutes** (default **15**). You need a [connected payment gateway](/en/pagos/cobro-in-chat/) (Mercado Pago, Flow or Khipu in Chile; Stripe or PayPal for international payments).

### How the customer experiences it

<Steps>

1. The customer picks a time and the agent **holds the slot** — but it stays **"awaiting payment"**, not yet **confirmed**. That slot is **blocked** so nobody else can take it.

2. The agent sends the **payment link** in the same chat and tells them how long they have to pay.

3. **If they pay in time** → the appointment **confirms itself**, gets booked, and the [reminders](/en/agendamiento/recordatorios/) are scheduled. The customer receives the confirmation with the address.

4. **If they don't pay** within the defined minutes → the slot is **released** automatically and becomes available to others.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
A service **without** "Charge to confirm" books directly, as always — the charge is optional per service. You can have some services that charge and others that don't.
</Aside>

### Useful details

- **The appointment confirms automatically when the payment is approved** — nothing manual for you to do.
- If the customer **pays just after** the hold expired and the slot is **still free**, the appointment is **re-confirmed** anyway. If someone else took the slot in the meantime, a **flag is left for your team to review** (reschedule or refund) — the customer is never charged twice.
- Everything is **traceable**: the payment stays linked to the appointment.

<Aside type="caution">
The charge requires a connected gateway and that the contact has an **email** (the agent asks for it before generating the link). If it's missing, the agent requests it first.
</Aside>
