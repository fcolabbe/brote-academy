Brote's scheduling lets you take **appointments and reservations directly over WhatsApp**, without the customer leaving the chat and without paying for an external tool like Calendly. It completes the brand's triad: **sell, schedule and collect payments over WhatsApp**.

## How it works

1. You define your **services** (e.g. "Haircut", 30 min) and your weekly **availability**.
2. The customer asks for a slot over WhatsApp → the **AI agent** offers genuinely free times, books and confirms.
3. Brote schedules **automatic WhatsApp reminders** before the appointment.
4. (Optional) You can also share a **public booking link** so the customer books on their own, from your website, your Instagram bio or an email.

```
Cliente: "¿Tienen hora para un corte el jueves?"
Agente:  "Sí 🙌 Tengo jueves 15:00, 15:40 o 17:00. ¿Cuál te queda?"
Cliente: "15:00"
Agente:  "Listo, te agendé el jueves a las 15:00. Te mando un recordatorio antes 🌱"
```

## Why native (and not Calendly / GoHighLevel)

- **No extra subscription**: included in your Brote plan, you don't pay another per-seat tool.
- **Inside the chat**: the customer doesn't jump to an external page — closer, better conversion.
- **Integrated**: the appointment is linked to the contact, the conversation and (if it has a price) you can charge it with the same payment flow.
- **Anti double-booking** guaranteed at the database level.

<Aside type="note">
Scheduling is available from the **Starter** plan and up.
</Aside>

## The three ways to book

| Path | When it's used |
|-----|---------------|
| **AI agent over WhatsApp** | The customer is already chatting and asks for a slot |
| **Public link** | You share it on your website/IG/email; the customer books on their own |
| **Manual** | You enter an appointment from Settings → Scheduling |
