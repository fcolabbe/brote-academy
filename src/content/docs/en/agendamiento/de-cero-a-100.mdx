## In one sentence

**Scheduling** = your customers **book their slot over WhatsApp by chatting with your AI agent**, which offers only genuinely free times, confirms the appointment, and sends reminders — all on its own, without you replying by hand.

You define **what can be booked** (your services) and **when** (your availability). From there, when a customer writes *"do you have an opening on Thursday?"*, the agent shows them the free slots, books, confirms, and reminds them before the appointment. **No Calendly, no extra tool to pay for, no leaving the chat.**

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this feature answers">
"I spend all day answering WhatsApp to hand out appointments. Can someone **take the bookings for me** without my calendar filling up with gaps or appointments falling through?"
</Aside>

## Who is this feature for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| You work **by appointment**: hair salons, barbershops, spas, nails | You don't take appointments or reservations (pure walk-in sales) |
| You're a **professional who sells your time**: physical therapist, psychologist, lawyer, dentist, veterinarian | You serve customers first-come, first-served with no calendar |
| You run a **practice or clinic** with patients requesting appointments | There's nothing to reserve in advance |
| You rent **lodging by the night**: cabins, hotel, guesthouse | — |

**Plainly put:** it's for any business that **lives off its calendar** and today loses time (and customers) handing out appointments by hand over WhatsApp.

<Aside type="caution" title="Plan required: Starter (Brote) and up">
Scheduling is available **from the Starter plan**. On Mini you'll see the section with a plan-required notice; upgrading to Starter or higher unlocks it. It's a feature included in your plan — **you don't pay for Calendly or any other separate subscription**.
</Aside>

## What do you need before you start?

<Steps>

1. **WhatsApp connected in Brote** — it's where the customer requests the slot and where reminders go out. ([How to connect it](/en/canales/whatsapp/)).

2. **At least one service created** — what the customer can book. Each service has a name, duration and, optionally, buffer, price and address. (E.g.: "Haircut", 30 min).

3. **Your weekly availability** — which hours can be booked, per day. You can set multiple blocks (e.g. morning and afternoon with a lunch break).

4. *(Optional)* **Google Calendar connected** — so appointments are written to your calendar and the agent **never books over events you already have**. Connects in one click. ([How](/en/integraciones/calendar/)).

5. *(Optional)* **A payment gateway** — if you want to **charge to confirm** the appointment and cut no-shows (Mercado Pago, Flow or Khipu in Chile; Stripe or PayPal for international payments).

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Everything is configured in one place: **Settings → Scheduling**. One service + availability is enough for the agent to start taking bookings.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Peluquería Estilo ✂️

> **Peluquería Estilo** is a neighborhood salon with 2 stylists. Before, Camila (the owner) answered WhatsApp between haircuts: she missed messages, gave out times that were already taken, and every week **3 or 4 customers didn't show** because nobody reminded them. Here's how she set it up with Brote.

<Steps>

1. **Camila adds her services** in Settings → Scheduling: "Haircut" (30 min), "Cut + beard" (45 min), "Color" (90 min). She sets a price for each.

2. **She sets her availability:** Monday to Friday 10:00–13:00 and 15:00–19:00, Saturday 10:00–14:00, Sunday closed. Brote will only offer times within those blocks, minus appointments already taken.

3. **She adds her two stylists** as resources ("Nico" and "Vale") and marks which services each one handles. Now two customers can take 4:00 PM the same day with different stylists, without clashing.

4. **Ana writes to the salon's WhatsApp:** *"Hi! Do you have an opening for a haircut Thursday afternoon?"*
   The AI agent replies on its own, right away:
   *"Yes 🙌 For Thursday I have 15:00, 15:40 or 17:20. Which works for you?"*

5. **Ana replies "17:20 with Vale".** The agent books the slot, blocks it so nobody else can take it, and confirms:
   *"Done Ana, I booked your Haircut for Thursday at 17:20 with Vale. I'll send you a reminder beforehand 🌱"*

6. **24 hours before**, Brote sends Ana, automatically:
   *"🌱 Hi Ana, a reminder of your Haircut tomorrow Thursday at 17:20. If you need to reschedule or cancel, reply to this message."* And another reminder **2 hours before**.

7. **Ana can't make it and replies to the reminder:** *"can I move it to Friday same time?"*. The agent reschedules on its own, frees up Thursday, and moves the reminders to Friday. Camila never even noticed — and Thursday's slot opened up for another customer.

</Steps>

**A month later, Camila runs the numbers:**

| | Before (by hand) | With Brote |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings handled over WhatsApp | Whenever she managed to reply | Automatic, 24/7 |
| Double-bookings | 1–2 per week | 0 (impossible by design) |
| No-shows | ~3–4 per week | ~1 (reminders eliminated the rest) |
| Camila's time on the phone | ~1 h per day | Almost none |

**The math that convinced her:** she went from losing ~3 bookings per week to gaps and no-shows to almost none. At an average ticket of $12.000, that's **~$140.000 a month** that used to slip away — plus her time back. All from entering her services and hours once.

Your calendar fills **on its own** while you work. The customer books in the same chat (better than sending them to an external page), there are never two overlapping appointments, and reminders make sure people **show up**.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. The only "task" is entering your services and hours **once** in Settings → Scheduling — literally filling in name, duration, and which blocks you work. From then on **you touch nothing**: the agent takes bookings, confirms, reminds, reschedules and cancels on its own. Connecting Google Calendar (optional) is one click. Charging to confirm (optional) is ticking a checkbox on the service.

<Aside type="note" title="How long does it take to get running?">
Less than 10 minutes: create your services and enter your availability. No technical knowledge needed.
</Aside>

## The three ways to book

With the same configuration, your customers can book through three paths:

| Path | When it's used |
|---|---|
| **AI agent over WhatsApp** | The customer is already chatting and asks for a slot — the agent books it in the conversation |
| **Public booking link** | A link you share in your Instagram bio, website or email; the customer books on their own, no chat and no account ([see](/en/agendamiento/link-publico/)) |
| **Manual** | You enter an appointment by hand from Settings → Scheduling |

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to pay for Calendly or another tool?**
No. Scheduling comes **included** in your Brote plan (Starter and up). You don't pay another subscription or per seat.

**Can the agent book over a meeting I already have?**
Not if you connect Google Calendar. Brote reads your availability (free/busy) before offering a slot, so it only proposes what's genuinely free. Block personal time in your Google calendar and the agent respects it.

**What if I have several professionals or rooms?**
Each one can have their own hours and handle only certain services. The agent handles "I want Dr. Rojas" and also "who's free on Tuesday?". See [Charge for the booking and per-professional calendar](/en/agendamiento/servicios-profesionales/).

**Can I charge for the appointment upfront?**
Yes. Check **💳 Charge to confirm** on the service: the slot stays reserved until the customer pays the link. If they don't pay within the defined time, it's released. It cuts no-shows dramatically.

**Does it work for renting cabins or rooms by the night?**
Yes, with **stay mode**: the customer books "July 10 to 14" instead of "3:00 PM", you charge per night, and there's seasonal pricing. See [Nightly lodging](/en/agendamiento/alojamiento-por-noches/).

**What happens if the customer wants to change or cancel?**
They just reply in the chat ("can we move it to Friday?") and the agent reschedules or cancels on its own, adjusting the reminders. You do nothing.

**Do reminders use up messages from my plan?**
Yes, they count as outbound messages, but they're low volume (one or two per appointment), so you'll rarely notice. You need WhatsApp connected for them to go out.
