All the configuration lives in **Settings → Scheduling**.

<Aside type="note">
Available from the **Starter** plan. On lower plans you'll see the section with a plan-required notice.
</Aside>

## 1. Create a service

A service is what the customer can book. Each one has:

- **Name** — e.g. "Haircut", "Photo session", "On-site visit".
- **Duration** (minutes) — how long the appointment lasts.
- **Buffer** (minutes) — rest/cleanup time between appointments. It's added to the step between time slots.
- **Price** (optional) — if you set it, you can later charge it with the [payment link](/en/pagos/cobro-in-chat/).
- **Address** (optional) — where the service happens; the agent shares it with the customer when booking.

Fill in the fields and click **Add**. You can have multiple services.

<Aside type="tip">
Professional time-based service (consultations, advisory, sessions)? You can **charge to confirm** the appointment and give **each professional their own calendar**. See [Charge for the booking and per-professional calendar](/en/agendamiento/servicios-profesionales/).
</Aside>

## 2. Set your weekly availability

You define which hours appointments can be booked, per day of the week. For each day you can add **one or more time blocks** with the **+ block** button.

```
Lunes a Viernes:  10:00–13:00  y  15:00–18:00   (corte de almuerzo)
Sábado:           10:00–14:00
Domingo:          cerrado
```

When you're done, click **Save availability**. Brote computes the actual free slots = availability − appointments already taken − buffer, respecting your timezone.

<Aside type="caution">
"Save availability" **replaces** all previous availability with what's on screen. To adjust, edit the blocks and save again.
</Aside>

## 3. Connect Google Calendar (optional)

From **Settings → Scheduling** you can connect your Google account in one click. Brote uses its own OAuth connection with Google Calendar, so you just authorize and you're done.

Connecting gives you two-way sync:

- **Appointments booked by the agent are written to your Google Calendar**, in a dedicated calendar called **"Brote — Citas"**. That way you see your bookings alongside the rest of your schedule.
- **Brote reads your availability (free/busy) from your primary calendar** before offering a time slot, so the agent **never books over events you already have**.

<Aside type="tip">
Block personal time or meetings in your Google calendar and the agent will respect it automatically: it only offers slots that are truly free.
</Aside>

## 4. View the schedule

On the same page, **Upcoming appointments** shows confirmed bookings. From there you can mark them **completed** or **cancel** them (cancelling frees the slot and deletes pending reminders).

## Done

With at least one active service + availability, you can now:
- Let the [agent book over WhatsApp](/en/agendamiento/que-es/), and
- Share your [public booking link](/en/agendamiento/link-publico/).
