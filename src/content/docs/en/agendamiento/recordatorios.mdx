Every time an appointment is booked (by the agent, through the public link, or by hand), Brote **schedules automatic WhatsApp reminders** to cut no-shows.

## How it works

- By default, reminders are scheduled **24 hours** and **2 hours** before the appointment.
- They're sent over **WhatsApp** to the contact's phone.
- If the appointment is **rescheduled**, the reminders are rescheduled to the new time.
- If it's **cancelled**, pending reminders are deleted.

```
🌱 Hola Camila, te recordamos tu cita de Corte de pelo el jueves 12 de junio, 15:00.
Si necesitas reagendar o cancelar, responde este mensaje.
```

## Requirements

- Your org needs **WhatsApp connected** ([how to connect it](/en/canales/whatsapp/)) for the reminder to go out. If it isn't, the appointment is still booked, just without the automatic heads-up.
- The contact needs a **phone number** (the public link always asks for it).

<Aside type="note">
Reminders count as outbound messages within your [plan's messaging limits](/en/campanas/caps/). They're low volume (one or two per appointment), so you'll rarely notice.
</Aside>

## Rescheduling and cancelling

The customer can simply **reply to the reminder** ("can I move it to Friday?") and the AI agent reschedules or cancels in the conversation, adjusting the reminders on its own.
