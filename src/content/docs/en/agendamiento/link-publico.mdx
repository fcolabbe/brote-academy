Besides booking over WhatsApp, Brote gives you a **public booking page**: a link any customer can open to grab a slot on their own, without being in a conversation and without creating an account.

## Your link

```
https://brote.ai/es/book/<slug-de-tu-negocio>
```

The `slug` is your business identifier. Share it wherever you have traffic:

- **Instagram / TikTok bio**
- **"Book now" button on your website**
- **Email signature** or a campaign email
- **Google Business Profile**

## What the customer sees

1. They pick the **service** (if you have more than one).
2. They pick a genuinely free **time slot**.
3. They fill in **name + phone** (email optional — required if the service charges).
4. They confirm → they see "Appointment confirmed!" and get their [WhatsApp reminder](/en/agendamiento/recordatorios/). If the service requires payment, they go through the payment step first (see below).

The appointment is recorded as a contact + conversation in your Inbox, just as if they had messaged you.

## Embed in your website (iframe)

To embed the booking inside your own page, use the `?embed=1` parameter (it removes the header/footer so it looks clean inside the iframe):

```html
<iframe
  src="https://brote.ai/es/book/<slug-de-tu-negocio>?embed=1"
  width="100%"
  height="640"
  style="border:0;border-radius:16px"
></iframe>
```

## Services with required payment

If your service has **"charge on booking"** enabled, the public link requires payment to confirm — just like the agent in the chat:

1. The customer picks a time and leaves their details (email becomes required, for the receipt).
2. Their slot is **held for 15 minutes** with a visible countdown.
3. They pay with the link shown (Mercado Pago, Flow, Khipu, Stripe or PayPal — depending on what you have connected).
4. When the payment is approved, the page switches on its own to "Appointment confirmed!" and reminders are activated.

If they don't pay in time, the hold expires and **the slot is offered again** to other customers — no ghost appointments clogging your calendar.

<Aside type="caution" title="You need a connected gateway">
A service with required payment but no connected gateway can't be booked through the public link (the customer sees a notice to contact you directly). Connect [Mercado Pago](/en/pagos/mercado-pago/), Flow or Khipu before enabling the charge.
</Aside>

## Anti double-booking

If two people try the same slot, **only one gets it** — the system guarantees it at the database level and offers the second person another time. Taken slots disappear from the list automatically.

<Aside type="note">
Public booking requires the **Starter** plan or higher and at least one service + availability configured. Otherwise, the link shows "Bookings not available".
</Aside>
