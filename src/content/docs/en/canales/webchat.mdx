Brote's webchat is a JavaScript widget you paste before `</body>` and it starts landing real conversations in your inbox. The AI agent replies to unknown visitors without them having to create an account.

![Embeddable webchat configuration](/screenshots/webchat-config.png)

## Why it matters

- **No third-party dependency**: it's not Tidio, Intercom or Crisp. The widget is served from your domain or `brote.ai`, and the data lives in your organization.
- **Same AI as on WhatsApp**: the agent replies with the same persona, the same catalogs, the same business rules.
- **Anonymous visitors = anonymous leads**: if the visitor leaves an email/phone during the conversation, they become an identified contact and enter your pipeline.

## How to connect it

1. Go to **Settings → AI Agent → Webchat**.
2. Copy the snippet (a `<script>` with your per-org widget key).
3. Paste it before `</body>` on your site (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, custom site — works anywhere).
4. Reload your site: the floating button appears at the bottom right. Click → chat with your AI agent.

## Customization

From the settings you can adjust:

- **Primary color and background** of the floating button
- **Agent avatar and name** (pulled automatically from onboarding)
- **Welcome message** (inherited from the AI agent, same prompt for all channels)
- **Position** of the button (left/right)
- **Visibility** by hour and day (e.g.: only 9-18, hidden on Sundays)

## When a visitor becomes a contact

If the agent asks for an email/phone and the visitor provides them, Brote creates an identified contact. Until then, they appear as "Web visitor" in the inbox + pipeline. Once identified, all future conversations from that device are linked to the same contact.

<Aside type="tip" title="Combine with Webhooks">
If you need a newly identified contact to trigger something external (HubSpot, Sheets, Mailchimp), connect [Zapier](/en/integraciones/zapier/) or [Business Webhooks](/en/integraciones/webhooks-api/) — every `new_contact` or `qualify_lead` is posted signed with HMAC.
</Aside>
