Brote connects to **Facebook Messenger** through **Meta's official channel** (direct). It works on top of your Facebook Business Page — the messages your page receives are handled by the AI agent just like on WhatsApp or Webchat.

![Integrations hub showing Facebook Messenger](/screenshots/integrations.png)

<Aside type="note" title="Available from Starter">
Messenger is part of the **Starter** ($49/mo), **Pro** ($129/mo) and **Business** ($349/mo) plans, alongside Instagram. On Mini the agent only operates on WhatsApp + Webchat. The official channels are the same from Starter up. See the [comparison](/en/planes/comparativa/).
</Aside>

## Before you start

- Your Facebook Page has to be **connected to a Business Manager account**.
- You have to be a **page admin** to authorize.
- If the page gets few messages, consider grouping Messenger with Instagram (Meta unifies them as "Meta Inbox") — Brote manages them as two separate channels with the same agent.

## How to connect

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Messenger**.
2. Click **"Connect Messenger"**. It redirects you to Meta's official login.
3. Log in with your Facebook account (the one that administers the page).
4. Choose the page you want to connect.
5. Authorize the permissions: read messages, send messages, manage Pages.
6. You return to Brote and the account shows as **connected**.

## Meta's limitations (same as IG)

Messenger has rules similar to Instagram's:

- You only reply if the user **wrote first**.
- After the user's first message you have **24h** to reply without restriction.
- Past the 24h, only "marketing" if the user accepted a subscription.
- No proactive mass sending outside the window.

## Typical cases in LATAM

- **Click-to-Messenger ads**: you build an ad in Meta Ads Manager with a "Send Message" CTA → leads land in Brote.
- **Comments with automatic replies**: when someone comments on a post asking for info, Meta allows your page to send them an automatic DM. Brote treats that DM as the start of a conversation.
- **Social commerce**: if you have a catalog on your page, the agent can share products in the chat (cart link via [Shopify](/en/integraciones/shopify/)).

## Conversion attribution (Business)

With the **Business** plan ($349/mo), Brote closes the ROAS loop on Messenger too:

- It captures the ad click (**Click-to-Messenger**) when the lead lands in the conversation.
- When the customer pays (Mercado Pago / Flow / Khipu), Brote fires the **Purchase** event to Meta via the Conversions API (CAPI) — and to TikTok too.
- That way you measure which ads actually generate sales, not just clicks.

Attribution (Meta + TikTok CAPI) works on all three official channels: WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger. It's exclusive to the Business plan.
