## In one sentence

A **channel** is **where your customers message you** — WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or your own website — and where **your AI agent replies to them on its own**, 24/7, with the same tone, catalog and prices.

You connect the channels where your people already are. Every incoming message — whichever channel it comes from — lands in a **unified inbox**, the AI agent replies in seconds and the contact is created automatically. One agent covering all your entry points.

<Aside type="tip" title="The question this guide answers">
"My customers message me on WhatsApp, DM me on Instagram and visit my website. How do I serve them **all** without being glued to my phone?"
</Aside>

## Who is this guide for?

| It's for you if… | NOT for you (yet) if… |
|---|---|
| Your customers message you on **WhatsApp, Instagram DM or Messenger** | You sell 100% in person and nobody messages you |
| You have a **website** and want to serve visitors | You have no digital channels or website |
| You keep answering the same things and **can't keep up** | You already have a large support team covering everything |
| You want **one inbox** instead of jumping between apps | You prefer to keep replying manually app by app |

**Plainly put:** it's for the business that **gets messages in several places** and wants an AI agent handling them all from one spot.

<Aside type="note" title="Which plan you need">
- **Webchat** (your website) and **WhatsApp** are available **from the Mini plan** — the two base channels every business starts with.
- **Instagram DM** and **Messenger** (Meta's official channels) are available **from Starter ($49/mo)** and up.
- Once on your plan, you connect **all the channels your plan includes at no extra cost per channel** — one business, its channels. See the [plan comparison](/en/planes/comparativa/).
</Aside>

## What do you need before connecting?

<Steps>

1. **Your Brote account** with the AI agent already configured (tone, catalog, rules). If you haven't set it up, [configure your first agent](/en/empezar/primer-agente/) first — it's what replies on every channel.

2. **Know where your people message you today.** WhatsApp? Instagram DMs? Your website? That decides what to connect first (no need to connect everything at once).

3. **For official WhatsApp:** a **number without an active WhatsApp** (ideally, a new dedicated SIM) and a Facebook account for the connection step.

4. **For Instagram / Messenger:** an Instagram **Business account** or a **Facebook Page** connected to a Business Manager, and being an **admin** of that account/page.

5. **For Webchat:** access to **paste a snippet** into your site (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or a custom site — it works on any of them).

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
You don't need any technical knowledge. Each channel connects with a few clicks: WhatsApp/Instagram/Messenger authorize through a Meta window, and Webchat is copy-paste a code snippet. **No channel charges extra per message** — messaging is included in your Brote plan.
</Aside>

## Real example: from zero to 100 with Camila 🌱

> **Camila** runs **"Deco Nube"**, a home decor store. She sells on **Instagram** (where she posts her products) and **WhatsApp** (where she closes sales), and recently launched her **website**. On her own, she couldn't keep up answering the same "do you have stock? do you ship?" in three different places.

<Steps>

1. **Camila connects WhatsApp first.** She goes to Settings → WhatsApp, taps "Connect official WhatsApp", the Meta window opens, she picks a new dedicated SIM, verifies with the 6-digit code and done. **5 minutes.** Her AI agent starts answering incoming WhatsApp messages right away.

2. **She adds Instagram.** In Settings → Integrations → Instagram she taps "Connect Instagram", authorizes with her Business account and it's connected. Now the DMs coming in from her posts are answered by **the same agent**, with the same catalog and prices.

3. **She pastes the Webchat into her site.** She copies the snippet from Settings → AI Agent → Webchat, pastes it before `</body>` on her site and the floating button appears. Website visitors now chat with the agent without creating an account.

4. **Everything lands in one inbox.** WhatsApp, Instagram and Webchat land in the **unified inbox**, filterable by channel. Camila opens a single inbox instead of three apps.

5. **The agent replies the same in all three.** Someone asks about a painting via Instagram DM → the agent shows the price ($25.000) and sends a payment link. Another asks about shipping in the webchat → same answer. Camila touches nothing.

6. **Contacts create themselves.** Every person who writes enters the pipeline as a contact. If they leave an email or phone, they're identified and their next messages — from whichever channel — are linked to the same contact.

</Steps>

**After the first week with all 3 channels connected, Camila sees:**

| Channel | Conversations | Answered by the agent | Camila stepped in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💬 WhatsApp | 84 | 78 | 6 |
| 📸 Instagram DM | 61 | 57 | 4 |
| 🌐 Webchat | 29 | 27 | 2 |
| **Total** | **174** | **162 (93%)** | **12** |

**What Camila achieved:** from replying by hand in 3 different apps to **one inbox where the agent answers 93% on its own**. She only steps in for the 12 cases that truly need her (a complaint, a special order). The rest — prices, stock, shipping, payment links — the agent resolves 24/7, even while she sleeps.

<Aside type="tip" title="This is what you get">
You stop losing sales to "I didn't see the message in time". Your customer writes wherever is convenient — WhatsApp, Instagram, your website — and **always gets a reply in seconds**. One agent, all your doors, one inbox.
</Aside>

## The 4 channels — what is each one for?

Connect the ones you use. You can have several at once, all with the same agent.

| Channel | What it's for | Ideal if… | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💬 **WhatsApp** (Cloud API) | The king channel in LATAM: where sales close | Your business already lives on WhatsApp | Mini+ |
| 📸 **Instagram DM** | Answering DMs from your posts, stories and ads | You sell/post on Instagram | Starter+ |
| 👍 **Messenger** | Handling messages to your Facebook Page | You have a community or customers on Facebook | Starter+ |
| 🌐 **Webchat** | A chat button on your own website, no third parties | You have a site and want to serve visitors | Mini+ |

<Aside type="note" title="Which one do I connect first?">
Start where **you get the most messages today**. For most LATAM businesses that's **WhatsApp**. Then add **Instagram** if you post there, and the **Webchat** as soon as you have a website. You don't need all four on day one — add them as you need them.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Details on each channel">
- **WhatsApp** connects via Meta's **official Cloud API** (no QR scanning, stable connection). Evolution remains an unofficial alternative.
- **Instagram and Messenger** have Meta's **24-hour rule**: the agent can reply freely within 24h of the customer writing first. Brote respects that window automatically.
- **Webchat** doesn't depend on any third party (it's not Tidio or Intercom): the widget is served from your domain and the data lives in your organization.
</Aside>

## How easy is it?

Very easy. None of the four channels requires technical knowledge:

- **WhatsApp:** 5 to 10 minutes. You authorize through a Meta window and verify with a 6-digit code. No QR.
- **Instagram / Messenger:** under 2 minutes each. A "Connect" button, you authorize with your Meta account and done.
- **Webchat:** copy and paste a snippet before `</body>`. If you can paste code into your website (or someone on your team can), you're set.

Everything is managed from **Settings → Integrations** (or Settings → AI Agent for the Webchat). Nothing to install, no servers.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to connect all four channels?**
No. Connect only the ones you use. Most people start with WhatsApp, add Instagram if they post there, and add the Webchat once they have a website. You add them as you need them.

**Is it the same agent on every channel?**
Yes. You configure your AI agent once (tone, catalog, rules) and it replies the same on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Webchat. No per-channel configuration.

**Does each channel charge separately?**
No. Messaging is included in your **Brote plan** — you don't set up payments with Meta or pay extra per channel. One plan, all the channels it includes.

**Can I reply myself whenever I want?**
Yes. Everything lands in the [unified inbox](/en/inbox/inbox-unificado/). The agent replies on its own, but you can take over any conversation manually or [hand off to a human](/en/inbox/handoff/) when the case calls for it.

**Why can I only reply on Instagram/Messenger if they message me first?**
It's a Meta rule, not Brote's: the agent can reply freely within **24h** of the customer writing to you. Outside that window, Meta restricts proactive messages to prevent spam. Brote respects that window automatically.

**Do I need a website to use the Webchat?**
Yes, the Webchat is a chat button that lives on your site. If you don't have a website yet, start with WhatsApp and Instagram.

**What about email and Telegram?**
They're on the roadmap as native channels, but **not available yet**. For now, serve customers with the four that are in production.
