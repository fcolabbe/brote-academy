<Aside type="caution" title="Coming soon">
The native **email channel** isn't available yet. It's on Brote's roadmap and we're presenting it here so you know what's coming. In the meantime, the official channels you can use today are [WhatsApp](/en/canales/whatsapp/), [Instagram](/en/canales/instagram/) and [Messenger](/en/canales/messenger/), plus the [embeddable webchat](/en/canales/webchat/).
</Aside>

We're building a **Brote-native email channel** (first-party email, no external provider dependencies) so your AI agent can read incoming emails, reply as if it were a human on your team, and keep the thread intact.

## What the agent will do with email

When we launch the channel, the AI agent will be able to:

- **Reply to incoming emails** keeping the subject + thread (correct `In-Reply-To` header).
- **Detect intent**: quote request, support, inquiry, complaint, new lead.
- **Create a ticket** in your pipeline just like on other channels.
- **Handle attachments**: if the customer attaches a photo/PDF, the agent stores it as context.
- **Hand off to a human** with a summary when it's a complex case (formal complaint, enterprise proposal, legal).

## In the meantime

Native email isn't available yet. If you want to chat 1:1 with your customers today, use the channels already in production:

- [WhatsApp](/en/canales/whatsapp/) — via Meta's official Cloud API.
- [Instagram](/en/canales/instagram/) and [Messenger](/en/canales/messenger/) — Meta's official channel, direct.
- [Embeddable webchat](/en/canales/webchat/) — for your website.

<Aside type="note" title="Telegram is coming too">
Like email, **Telegram** is a native channel we're preparing. Both will arrive as first-party Brote channels, not third-party integrations.
</Aside>

## For mass campaigns

The email channel will be for 1:1 conversations. If what you want is to message your contact base, use [Campaigns](/en/campanas/que-son/) over WhatsApp.
