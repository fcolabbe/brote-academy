Brote will offer **Telegram** as a native channel in the future, via the **Bot API (official Telegram)**. The AI agent will operate as a bot for your business that anyone can chat with by starting a conversation with your username.

<Aside type="caution" title="Coming soon">
Telegram is **not available yet**. We're building the native channel with Telegram's **official Bot API**. In the meantime, serve your customers over **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**, **Messenger** and **Webchat**. See the [plan comparison](/en/planes/comparativa/).
</Aside>

## When Telegram will make sense

- **Your vertical is tech**: software, crypto, dev tools, startups → your audience already lives on Telegram.
- **You run communities**: you have a Telegram group with customers/prospects and want a bot answering questions.
- **You're in LATAM**: Telegram has strong penetration in Argentina and Chile among younger audiences.

If your ideal customer is a traditional SMB in retail/hospitality/health → you probably don't need Telegram. WhatsApp + Webchat cover it well.

## What the native channel will bring

When Telegram is available, the bot will work like this:

- **/start**: the bot will reply with the AI agent's welcome message.
- **Free text**: the AI agent will reply like on any other channel (same prompt, same catalog, same tone).
- **Media**: the bot will be able to receive and send photos, videos, audio and documents — all in your [unified inbox](/en/inbox/inbox-unificado/).
- **Commands**: fixed commands (`/precios`, `/horarios`, `/contacto`) configurable from Brote.
- **Groups vs DMs**: in 1:1 chats the agent replies to every message; in groups it only replies if explicitly mentioned (`@mibot ...`) or if someone replies to one of its messages, to avoid spamming large communities.
