Brote connects to WhatsApp via Meta's **official Cloud API** — the recommended path. You connect your number from Brote without scanning any QR, with the stability and backing of the official API. **Evolution API** remains available as an unofficial alternative (it pairs your number by scanning a QR, just like WhatsApp Web).

![WhatsApp channel configuration in Brote](/screenshots/whatsapp-config.png)

## Official Cloud API (recommended)

It's the recommended way to connect WhatsApp to Brote:

- **No QR.** You connect your number directly from Brote, without linking a phone or re-scanning anything.
- **Official stability.** Your number doesn't disconnect from inactivity or a phone change.
- **Meta's backing.** It's the official WhatsApp Business API, built for companies.

<Aside type="danger" title="Before you start: the number CANNOT have an active WhatsApp">
A number can be on the **WhatsApp app** *or* on the **official API** — never both. If the number you're going to connect already has WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, **delete that account first**: open WhatsApp on that phone → **Settings → Account → Delete my account**.

💡 Ideally, use a **new/dedicated number** for customer service (a new SIM works), so you don't lose your personal WhatsApp. **90% of connection problems come from skipping this step.**
</Aside>

### What you need

- Your Brote account on the **Starter plan or higher**, and being an **administrator**.
- A **Facebook account** (personal — only used for the connection step).
- A **number** that **doesn't have an active WhatsApp** and can **receive an SMS or call** (for the verification code).
- Your **business name** as you want your customers to see it.

### How to connect (Cloud API) — 5 to 10 minutes

1. Go to **Settings → WhatsApp** and tap **"Connect official WhatsApp"**.
2. A **Facebook/Meta** window opens — log in with your Facebook account.
3. Follow Meta's steps:
   - Choose or **create your business profile** (the same window creates it if you don't have one).
   - Enter the **display name** for your WhatsApp (what your customers will see).
   - Pick your business **category** and enter your **phone number**.
4. You'll receive a **6-digit code** by SMS or call → enter it to verify the number.
5. Accept the terms → **done!** 🎉 The window closes on its own and you'll see your number **connected** in Brote.

From that moment on, every message arriving at your WhatsApp shows up in your [unified inbox](/en/inbox/inbox-unificado/), the AI agent replies according to your configuration, and new contacts are created automatically.

<Aside type="note" title="Billing and display name">
Messaging is billed through your **Brote plan** — you don't set up payments with Meta directly. The **display name** is reviewed by Meta (it must represent your business, nothing generic or misleading); while it's being approved, you can still receive and reply to messages.
</Aside>

## Evolution API (unofficial alternative)

If you prefer a quick connection without going through the official API, Brote also supports **Evolution API**, an unofficial integration that pairs your number by scanning a QR — just like WhatsApp Web.

### How to connect (Evolution)

1. Go to **Settings → AI Agent → WhatsApp**.
2. Choose to connect via **Evolution** and a QR appears.
3. On your phone: open WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.
4. Scan the QR. Done — Brote is now replying on that number.

<Aside type="caution" title="Evolution's limitation">
Because it's an unofficial connection, **WhatsApp mass campaigns** respect per-plan caps (Starter 2/month · 100 contacts, Pro 10/month · 500, Business unlimited/month · 1500/day) to avoid triggering a Meta ban. More in [Caps per plan and WA protection](/en/campanas/caps/).
</Aside>

## Outbound messages from Brote

Your AI agent can start conversations — not just reply. Three paths:

- **Agent triggers** (after a Shopify event, MP payment, cart abandonment).
- **[Mass campaigns](/en/campanas/que-son/)** to cohorts of your contact base.
- **API/Webhooks/Zapier** from external systems ([Zapier](/en/integraciones/zapier/), [Business Webhooks](/en/integraciones/webhooks-api/)).

## Multimedia

The AI agent can send **photos, videos, PDFs and audio** from your [Media library](/en/inbox/tags-fields/). Useful for sending catalogs, how-tos, product sheets or demo videos.

## If the number disconnects (Evolution)

With Evolution, WhatsApp can close the session due to inactivity or a phone change. Brote notifies you by email + push notification. Re-scanning the QR takes 30s and you don't lose message history. With the official Cloud API this doesn't happen: the connection is stable and doesn't depend on a linked phone.
