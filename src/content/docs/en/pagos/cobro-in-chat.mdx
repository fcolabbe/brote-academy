In-chat payments are the feature that moves the needle the most. The AI agent detects when the customer wants to buy, calculates the amount, sends the [Mercado Pago](/en/pagos/mercado-pago/) link and handles the follow-up — all without you having to step in.

## The end-to-end flow

```
Cliente: "lo quiero llevar"
Agente IA: 
  → invoca build_payment_link({amount: 49000, description, ref})
  → recibe init_point URL de MP
  → manda link al cliente
"Listo, acá va el link de pago: https://mpago.la/abc123 
 Pagas con tarjeta, Mercado Pago o transferencia."

Cliente: [paga en MP]

[MP webhook → Brote → ticket actualizado]

Agente IA:
"Perfecto, recibe tu pago. Te llega en 2 días hábiles a tu dirección.
 Cualquier duda, escríbeme."
```

## How it detects intent

The AI agent detects buying intent from phrases like:

- "I want it", "I'll take it", "give me that"
- "how do I pay", "what's the payment link"
- "I've decided", "ok send it"
- Confirmation after a suggestion ("ok great, that one")

For ambiguous cases ("I'm interested") the agent asks first ("do you want to buy it now or think it over a bit more?") to avoid over-pressuring.

## Amount calculation

The AI agent calculates:

- **Base price** of the chosen products (via [Shopify](/en/integraciones/shopify/) if connected).
- **Quantity** the customer asks for.
- **Coupon** if the customer mentions one or if the conversation comes from a campaign with a pre-applied coupon.
- **Shipping** if you have a shipping cost table configured in agent_blocks.
- **Taxes** if your business includes them in the final amount (B2B usually invoices them separately).

If in doubt, the agent confirms the breakdown with the customer before sending the link: "Confirming: 1 XL box = $49.000 + shipping $3.500 = $52.500 total. Shall I proceed?".

## What the customer sees

Click the link → native MP checkout:

- Card, debit, MercadoPago wallet, bank transfer.
- Installments per your MP config (1-12 installments, with/without interest).
- Instant confirmation when they pay.

<Aside type="tip" title="Without leaving WhatsApp">
Mercado Pago has a web view optimized for WhatsApp — the customer pays without opening the browser. Removing that friction is what drives conversion.
</Aside>

## What happens after the payment

1. **MP posts a webhook** to Brote with `payment.id` + status `approved`.
2. **Brote records it** in `mercadopago_payments`, associates it with the `conversation_id`.
3. **Moves the pipeline ticket** to "Payment confirmed".
4. **Notifies the customer** automatically.
5. **If you have [Zapier](/en/integraciones/zapier/) active**: fires `payment_received` → your Holded creates an invoice, your Sheet logs it, your Shopify marks the order as paid.

## Recurring subscriptions

For subscription services (monthly membership, monthly pet food box, B2B SaaS), the agent can generate a **preapproval link** instead of a one-time payment. The customer accepts once, and MP charges automatically every month/week/whatever you configure.
