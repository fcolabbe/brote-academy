The **lifecycle stage** is the customer's stage in their relationship with your
business. It's orthogonal to the **ticket pipeline** (which is per conversation):
a person can be `customer` (already paid) and have an open ticket
in `nuevo` at the same time.

## Kanban pipeline vs Lifecycle funnel — the difference

Brote has two views that look alike but are different:

| | Kanban pipeline | Lifecycle funnel |
|---|---|---|
| **Per** | Ticket (one conversation) | Contact (the person) |
| **Stages** | Defined by the org (New, Qualified, Closed-won/lost…) | Brote canon (Lead → MQL → SQL → Opportunity → Customer → Evangelist → Churned) |
| **Multiplicity** | One person can have N tickets in N stages | One person = a single lifecycle stage |
| **Purpose** | Operational workflow of a specific conversation | The customer's relational stage with your business |
| **URL** | `/app/pipelines/...` | `/app/contacts/funnel` |

### Brote links the two automatically

You don't have to update the lifecycle manually when a ticket closes:

| When this happens | The contact's lifecycle updates like this |
|---|---|
| Ticket moves to a stage with `is_won = true` | Bumps from Lead/MQL/SQL/Opportunity → **Customer** |
| Payment approved in Mercado Pago, Flow or Khipu | Bumps to **Customer** |

If the contact is already `Customer`, `Evangelist` or `Churned`, **it's not downgraded** — Brote doesn't overwrite manual states with automatic ones.

The change is recorded in `contact_lifecycle_history` with `source = 'workflow'` to distinguish it from manual changes (`source = 'manual'`) and imports (`source = 'csv_import'`).

### With Meta CAPI connected

If your org has the Meta Conversions API connected, each lifecycle transition also fires a server-side event:

| Target stage | Meta event |
|---|---|
| MQL / SQL | `Lead` |
| Opportunity | `InitiateCheckout` |
| Customer | `Purchase` (via payment webhook) |
| Evangelist | `CompleteRegistration` |

This closes the full attribution funnel: Meta knows which ad generated each Lead, MQL, SQL, Opportunity and Purchase, not just the final conversion.

## Standard stages

| Stage | Color | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | gray | Entered the chat but hasn't qualified yet |
| **MQL** | blue | Marketing Qualified — interest expressed |
| **SQL** | violet | Sales Qualified — clear intent to buy |
| **Opportunity** | amber | Negotiating price/terms |
| **Customer** | green | Paid at least once |
| **Evangelist** | cyan | Customer who refers others |
| **Churned** | red | Lost or inactive customer |

## How to change the stage

### From the visual pipeline (`/app/contacts/funnel`)

The funnel's kanban view shows all your contacts grouped by
stage. **Drag a card from one column to another** to move the
contact. Stage-to-stage conversion (% moving from N → N+1) is
calculated live above each column.

Typical cases:
- Lead who replied with concrete interest → MQL.
- MQL who asked for a price → SQL.
- SQL you sent a quote to → Opportunity.
- Opportunity that paid → Customer.
- Customer inactive 3+ months → Churned.

The "No stage" column groups contacts you haven't classified yet.
Drag them from there to the right column.

Text filter above the board: search by name, phone, email
or tag. Speeds up work when you have 500+ contacts.

### From a conversation's side panel

In any Inbox conversation, the right-hand panel has a
"Customer stage" picker. Click → pick the new stage. Brote saves the
change and records who made it and when in
`contact_lifecycle_history` (for reporting + auditing).

### From a CSV import

Include a `lifecycle`, `stage` or `etapa` column in your CSV. Valid
values: `lead`, `mql`, `sql`, `opportunity`, `customer`, `evangelist`,
`churned`.

### From the AI agent (coming soon)

We're working on an agent tool `set_lifecycle_stage` so
the AI can move the contact when it detects clear signals (e.g. customer
says "I already paid" → move to `customer` automatically).

## Reporting

Stages feed:

- **Conversion funnel**: lead → mql → sql → customer (how many move
  from each stage to the next).
- **Average time in each stage**: to spot bottlenecks.
- **Campaign segmentation**: send a broadcast only to `customer` or
  exclude `churned`.

For now these views live in the general dashboard; in an upcoming
update we'll have a dedicated funnel at `/app/analytics/funnel`.

<Aside type="tip">
If your business has a different funnel (e.g. B2B SaaS with `trial → paid`),
you can ignore the standard stages and use only the ones that apply. It's not
mandatory to have all stages active.
</Aside>

## Change history

Every change is recorded:

```sql
contact_lifecycle_history (
  contact_id,
  from_stage,
  to_stage,
  changed_by,
  source ('manual' | 'csv_import' | 'workflow' | 'api'),
  created_at
)
```

Useful for auditing and for detecting reversals (customer coming back from
`churned` to `customer` = repurchase).
